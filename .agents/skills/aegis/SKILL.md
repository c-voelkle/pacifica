# Aegis

Turn a Pacifica decision cluster or proposed change into an evidence-backed comparison under the [success contract](references/success-contract.md). Read that contract and the shared [ontology workflow](references/ontology-workflow.md) at the start of a full review. Its target is Julian's effective power by 1932, not general national wealth or an imposed successful ending.

## Establish the comparison

Locate the Pacifica repository and follow its AGENTS.md and canonical read order. Pin the current master, approval register, character identities, relevant historical background and financial reference by commit and file hash. Resolve current remote refs when available; identify an unverified local snapshot explicitly.

Record the cluster, earliest divergence, feasible comparator, endpoint, checkpoints and scope of the requested review. Retaining the baseline is an alternative. A repair to exposition is not automatically a change in the world's outcomes. Preserve approved decisions, protected ambiguity and local PROPOSED, OPEN and MODEL qualifications.

## Trace power to Julian

For each material claim, identify the resource or capability, its holder at each stage, Julian's eventual instrument of control, conditions and refusal points. Distinguish legal authority, practical command, ownership, access, cooperation and personal influence. Trace creation, preservation, transmission, exercise and further accumulation, including interruption and succession. Do not inherit Elias's expired delegation into Julian's office.

Use exact storyline passages. Represent material propositions as dated, case-scoped Claims and their transmission as MechanismLinks. Keep each local authorial qualification attached to its claim. Mark each link as explained, asserted, missing or contradicted in the narrative; assess historical evidence and quantitative support separately. Explain necessary prerequisites, plausible competing causes, liabilities, rival adaptation and the prospect that a gain strengthens an independent institution instead of Julian.

Specify an action Julian can obtain through an office or institution, its instrument or instrument gap, conditions, refusal, expiry and burdens. Dependable cooperation can confer usable control without transferring ownership. An entitlement is not a receipt; accession is not a universal command instrument. Distinguish explanatory clarification from a new bargain or power in separate ComparisonCases and Judgments.

Actors pursue their own contemporary purposes. The author can evaluate a century-long consequence, but that knowledge does not establish an earlier actor's motive or available information.

## Investigate and develop alternatives

Prioritize questions that could reverse the recommendation. Use [Clio](../clio/SKILL.md) for bounded evidence research. At its research checkpoint, tell the author the question, available evidence, needed sources and decision consequence; invite titles or references. Continue independent work while dependent claims stay provisional. A source request is not a blanket approval gate.

Develop materially distinct alternatives where useful: baseline, minimal clarification/repair and a substantive change. Identify additions, removals and affected later events; do not assume later authored successes remain unchanged after changing their prerequisites. Retain unsuccessful alternatives with reasons.

Use [Chryseis](../chryseis/SKILL.md) for consequential quantities. Supply pinned baselines, dated changes, ownership, hypotheses to test and relevant missing inputs. Quantitative work may be partial; do not convert a financing calculation into a validated national-power result. Other feasibility calculations are scoped explicitly and remain separate from financial evidence.

## Challenge and recommend

Attempt to defeat the preferred argument: stronger private wealth without control, higher gross receipts with larger obligations, temporary command that expires, an empowered partner that can refuse, and a succession that breaks the chain. Reconsider a near-term restraint that may preserve Julian's eventual capacity. These are diagnostic cases, not facts to insert into the plot.

Deliver a readable assessment with:
- Baseline/comparator and contract version.
- A dated transmission chain through the endpoint, with passage references and control boundaries.
- Issues, source findings and unresolved dependencies.
- Comparable alternatives, burdens, executed calculations and sensitivities.
- Directional verdicts, claim basis and the evidence that could reverse them.
- A concrete recommendation and author decisions still needed.

Save the readable assessment, review Turtle, input manifest, supporting research/calculations and executed validation/query output in a unique task-owned review directory. Use the shared workflow to validate the actual review graph, inspect query findings and reconcile the prose with its material claim/link IDs before delivery. Retain unresolved dependencies and validation failures explicitly; structural conformance does not establish causal sufficiency. Applying recommendations to canon, changing approvals, publishing, committing or pushing requires the corresponding user authorization.
