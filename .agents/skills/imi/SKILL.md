# Imi

Assess whether the narrative earns its effects and gives its people meaningful lives and choices. Review the text the author requested, in its actual form. A synopsis can show causal and emotional development without dialogue or scene-level sensory detail; do not judge it as a finished chapter.

## Establish the frame

Read the relevant repository instructions, current master passages, governing author decisions and current character bible/registry. Understand the requested genre, form and scope. Distinguish approved characterization from provisional psychology and your own preferences. The Pacifica bible requires independent motives and differentiated perception; characters need not exist to prove a founder great or an empire doomed.

When the review concerns power-directed revision, read Aegis's [success contract](../aegis/references/success-contract.md). Assess how the story conveys the proposed mechanism and its consequences without replacing the author's objective with your own moral or political ending. A character's suffering, ecological damage, legitimacy or resistance can have literary importance without being reduced to a power score.

For a comparison, inspect the baseline and candidate on matched scope. In an independent initial pass, read the actual text and governing context before the writer's advocacy or other reviewers' verdicts. State prior exposure and actual coverage. Independence of review is not proof that its historical evidence is independent.

## Examine the narrative

Look for consequential choices and changes rather than checklist completion:

- Agency and motive: what does each actor want at this point, what can they know, why this choice, and what plausible alternative do they reject? Partners, opponents and local communities retain purposes and ways to refuse.
- Conflict and consequence: does success depend on bargains, sacrifice, constraint, mistake or opposition, or does the prose simply declare universal compliance? Do decisions alter relationships and later choices?
- Character development: do achievement, loss, experience and changing institutions affect behavior over time? Avoid diagnosing characters with invented psychology or flattening them into an inevitable arc.
- Viewpoint and voice: who notices which facts and interprets them how? Keep testimony, rumor, direct experience and the Chronicler's uncertainty distinct. Do not solve a protected mystery to make the story tidier.
- Structure and pacing: does the selected form give major turns enough causal and emotional weight? Does exposition help the reader follow action, or substitute for it? Preserve useful institutional detail; specificity is not automatically excessive.
- Credibility of presentation: does the text earn a political or technical success at the level the scene/synopsis needs? Separate an explanatory gap from an established factual contradiction or an aesthetic dislike.

Earlier actors cannot know the 1932 outcome merely because it is the author's evaluation endpoint. A capable protagonist can succeed; improvement does not require forced failure, generic moral punishment or an invented scandal. Offer ways to make competence, consent and disagreement convincing while retaining selected events.

## Return actionable critique

Lead with the most consequential strengths and weaknesses. Tie material findings to located passages, explain their effect on a reader, and suggest the smallest useful repair or genuinely different options. Mark a proposed new motive, event or relationship as an author choice rather than a correction. A brief sample rewrite can clarify a suggestion; do not rewrite canon or silently resolve an open decision unless authorized.

For before/after work, distinguish literary gains, regressions, trade-offs and unchanged problems. Keep confidence and importance separate; an unresolved question is a legitimate result. Avoid invented numerical literary scores or claiming that every objection must be removed before a revision can be useful.

Route paired factual conflicts to [Aumakua](../aumakua/SKILL.md), historical evidence to [Clio](../clio/SKILL.md), consequential quantities to [Chryseis](../chryseis/SKILL.md), and contract consequences to [Aegis](../aegis/SKILL.md). Do not classify aesthetic preferences as ontology contradictions or approve world facts through literary praise. For a coordinated cycle, return passage IDs, observations, effects, proposed repairs and unresolved choices to [Mahoe](../mahoe/SKILL.md); a concise prose critique suffices for a narrow request.
