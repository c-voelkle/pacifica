# Kalai

Turn a selected direction into actual narrative prose. Match the author's requested form: a continuous master synopsis, a standalone alternative or CR draft, chapter prose, or a narrow passage repair. Do not replace requested writing with an implementation memo or insist that a synopsis contain fully dramatized scenes.

## Establish the writing brief

Read repository AGENTS.md, the relevant current master passages and governing approvals. Use the current character bible and stable-ID registry for identity, motivation and voice. Apply later author corrections over earlier assistant suggestions or source snapshots. Preserve local APPROVED, PROPOSED, OPEN and MODEL distinctions; approval of a direction does not settle every implementation detail.

Identify selected changes, preserved premises, unresolved choices, narrative viewpoint and the authorized destination. A request to draft a CR or explore an alternative does not authorize altering the master, approvals or identities. A direct correction or integration instruction authorizes its stated scope; use existing authorization without asking again. Keep brainstorming intact until writing is requested.

For a power-directed revision, read Aegis's [success contract](../aegis/references/success-contract.md) and supplied comparison. Explain an existing mechanism without claiming it increases world power. A new legal right, bargain, institution or resource is a substantive premise requiring its own qualification and consequences.

## Write the change and follow its consequences

Read the affected prose and trace its setup and later uses. Make a compact impact map of additions, removals, date shifts, identities, powers, knowledge and quantities before a substantial rewrite. Extend that map through succession, breach, later crises and the agreed endpoint where affected. Combine ontology links with raw-text searches; an incomplete graph is not the limit of the task.

Write events as events: people pursue purposes with the information available to them, bargain, decide, resist, act and experience consequences. Institutional mechanisms should be legible through those actions. Keep historical comparisons, source arguments and editing explanations in supporting notes rather than canonical story prose. Preserve the book's differentiated viewpoints and the Chronicler's limits; narrator certainty must not silently replace testimony or deliberate ambiguity.

Propagate changed dates into ages, durations, anniversary language, terms, offices, travel and who could know or authorize an action. Propagate changed institutions into later permissions and refusal; changed commitments into their burdens and dependencies. Removing war or an event also requires reconsidering the debts, deaths, alliances and opportunities that depended on it. Do not preserve a later authored victory merely because it appeared in the baseline.

Do not invent a replacement event, off-page amendment, settled motive, murderer or genealogical link to make the draft close. Where a required bridge remains undecided, mark it explicitly OPEN at the appropriate level and give concrete alternatives in the notes. Finish the supported prose rather than reducing the deliverable to questions. Do not silently choose a consequential new premise to remove an OPEN marker.

Use [Clio](../clio/SKILL.md) for historical questions and [Chryseis](../chryseis/SKILL.md) for consequential quantitative changes. A saved workbook value is not a new calculation. Preserve supplied financial attachments and distinguish narrative changes from any later authorized model work. Do not insert an invented amount or probability merely to make the narrative sound complete.

## Review and deliver

Read the full affected sequence after editing, including untouched counterpart passages. Use [Aumakua](../aumakua/SKILL.md) for consistency, [Imi](../imi/SKILL.md) for literary review and Aegis for material contract consequences when the task calls for them. Resolve demonstrated regressions within scope; distinguish independent review from self-review and preserve remaining uncertainties.

For a draft, deliver the actual revised narrative or complete alternative plus concise impact notes: baseline, changed premises, affected passages/claims, unchanged assumptions and author choices still needed. Keep alternate cases distinct. Supply a diff when it helps inspection. Do not call a proposal adopted or a synopsis patch fully integrated while affected later prose still says otherwise.

For authorized canon integration, revise the actual master and downstream passages, update the current CR register and adjacent coverage IDs, keep historical research in its proper file, and update character prose/registry together where affected. Follow the repository's current synchronization and validation commands. These checks establish their stated conditions; they do not certify literary quality, history or comprehensive consistency. Preserve original attachments, legacy profiles and synced source material.
