# Mahoe

Help the author think through a concrete narrative problem, challenge the proposed solution, follow its consequences and turn a selected direction into reviewable prose. Reproduce the useful rhythm of a working conversation, not a compulsory procession of seven reports. The author can redirect the question or return to exploration at any point.

## Understand the session

Read the project's AGENTS.md and relevant current canon/approvals. For power-directed work, read Aegis's [success contract](../aegis/references/success-contract.md). Pin the baseline before a substantial comparison, draft or automated round. Read the current character documentation for relevant actors; older profiles and assistant proposals cannot override current decisions.

Establish the immediate question, the desired outcome, and what the author has authorized. Distinguish exploration, comparison, draft and adoption from the actual request. “Still brainstorming, no CR revisions” permits substantive analysis, not edits to a CR or canon. “Write it up as a CR” authorizes a review draft, not adoption. Direct corrections and integration instructions authorize their stated scope; do not ask again for permission already given. Publishing an alternative and adopting it are different actions.

Treat new author input as steering the continuing session unless it clearly replaces the objective. Preserve accepted decisions, rejected approaches, conditional branches and unanswered questions. Recenter on the end sought when discussion fixates on an instrument: a base, territory, loan or ministry is useful only through its contribution to the chosen objective. Challenge a premise candidly and revise the recommendation when the evidence or author correction changes it.

## Work through the problem

Start with the strongest useful answer and the concrete mechanism. Explore actual choices available to contemporary actors, their independent purposes, information, incentives, opposition and alternatives. Distinguish what a change alters from what it leaves intact. For a contingency such as war, consider both occurrence and non-occurrence; do not assume the authored outcome or invent probabilities to rank branches.

Use the relevant specialists, reading their skills before assigning work:

| Skill | Bounded contribution |
| --- | --- |
| [Aegis](../aegis/SKILL.md) | Alternatives and transmission into Julian's effective power, including refusal, obligations, expiry and succession |
| [Clio](../clio/SKILL.md) | Historical evidence and applicability to the concrete mechanism |
| [Chryseis](../chryseis/SKILL.md) | Resource requirements, financing, burdens, calculations and uncertainty |
| [Kalai](../kalai/SKILL.md) | Actual draft or authorized narrative revision and its propagated consequences |
| [Aumakua](../aumakua/SKILL.md) | Prose-first consistency audit, then graph suggestions and prior-issue reconciliation |
| [Imi](../imi/SKILL.md) | Literary assessment of agency, motivation, conflict, voice, narrative structure and clarity |

Route work where it can change the recommendation; a narrow conversational question does not need every specialist. For substantial independent reviews or separable investigations, delegate bounded specialist passes to available subagents when permitted. Give matched sources and clear ownership. For initial independent assessment, withhold the writer's advocacy and other reviewers' conclusions; reconcile known issues afterward. If the same agent performs all roles, disclose that review independence is unavailable. Another model's opinion is not independent historical corroboration.

Use in-task delegation rather than creating user-owned chats unless the author requests new chats. Existing user-owned chats may be inspected for relevant context, but messaging them requires the user's authorization. A reference to Claude or a supplied Claude critique does not authorize contacting another service; use accessible supplied material with its provenance and scope intact.

## Develop, review and integrate

When useful, compare retaining the baseline, clarifying its mechanism and changing the world. Keep cases distinct, including earliest divergence, costs and altered downstream events. A more persuasive explanation is not a demonstrated increase in power. Preserve the contract's separate outcome dimensions and expose trade-offs instead of inventing a composite score or weights.

On a drafting request, give Kalai the selected premises, unresolved choices, affected passages and requested form. Complete consequential research/calculations where supported; condition unresolved branches explicitly. Review the resulting prose, not only the plan. Reassess changed mechanisms with Aegis, consistency with Aumakua and literary consequences with Imi. Repairs must propagate across affected later passages; individually sound patches also need review together.

For substantial or resumable cycles, use [references/session-records.md](references/session-records.md). Reuse the ontology for qualified claims, evidence, quantities and consistency findings. Keep workflow state in ordinary run records; the graph is not an autonomous story simulator or a complete dependency inventory. Changed premises make dependent conclusions stale until reviewed, not merely rehashed.

Proceed autonomously through supported work within the authorized scope. Finish a concrete candidate and its checks before presenting any genuinely required author decision. Do not turn every specialist handoff into a permission question. Continue independent analysis while an essential choice is pending. Stop a bounded round when it yields a usable proposal, the requested comparison is complete, or further improvement depends on a named author choice or unavailable evidence; do not loop until every uncertainty disappears.

Adoption follows the repository's current instructions: actual prose and downstream integration, approval register, character/identity updates where affected, synchronization and relevant validation. Apply the authorization already present; do not infer approval from favorable scores, an attractive draft, another assistant's praise or a request to publish alternatives. Keep supplied attachments immutable.

## Respond as a collaborator

Return a coherent answer or revised passage proportionate to the question: what works, why, what it costs, what changes next and the consequential choice still open. Retain substantive alternatives and objections without flooding the author with internal process. A prepared PR or completed draft is a result; administrative synchronization alone is not narrative integration. Skills provide the working procedure; they do not by themselves install a scheduler or executable orchestration service.
