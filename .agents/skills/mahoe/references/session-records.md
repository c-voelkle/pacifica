# Durable records for a substantial development session

Use records when alternatives, multiple specialists, integration or a resumable run need them. A short brainstorming answer does not require a folder, schema or checklist. Keep working records outside canonical narrative and synced sources.

## Session brief

Record the author's question and desired outcome; baseline revision and important file hashes; current intent (explore, compare, draft, adopt); authorization already given and remaining scope boundaries. Retain consequential author corrections and the source of each decision. Record unavailable history honestly: related chat summaries, imported CRs and Claude excerpts are not a complete transcript of every prior session.

Use a small Markdown or JSON record with stable IDs, not a new ontology for task management. Separate author decisions, evidence, analyst hypotheses, literary preferences and quantitative estimates. A discussion of an option is not its approval; an author's selected draft may still contain local PROPOSED, OPEN and MODEL details.

## Candidate and impact record

For each material candidate record its case ID, baseline, earliest divergence, concrete additions/removals/date changes, affected actors/institutions/passages and unresolved premises. Retain the baseline option. Mark exposition-only changes separately from changes to the world. Follow consequences through the relevant endpoint; unchanged outcomes must be justified after changing their prerequisites.

Link to existing ontology Claims, MechanismLinks, EvidenceApplications, QuantityClaims and Aumakua findings where they exist. Keep missing extraction visible. Do not force Imi's aesthetic judgments into contradiction classes or imply that a graph path executes a causal mechanism.

Before a specialist handoff, provide the actual source passages, task, case/claim IDs, relevant authority, requested deliverable and permitted side effects. Give Clio the mechanism to investigate and Chryseis the comparison and missing quantities. Give Kalai the selected premises and requested writing form. Give reviewers the actual candidate text and governing context; withhold advocacy during the initial pass when independence is intended.

## Review and completion record

Keep three judgments distinct:

- Contract: improvement, weakening, trade-off or unresolved on each relevant power dimension; explanation-only gains labelled separately.
- Consistency: supported new conflicts, retained/resolved/not-reproduced earlier findings, actual reading and graph coverage, and unresolved integration.
- Literary: concrete effects on agency, motivation, conflict, viewpoint, voice, structure and clarity, relative to the requested form.

Record independent versus same-agent assessment, actual calculations/checks, evidence that changed the recommendation, rejected repairs and remaining decisions. A second opinion does not supply missing historical evidence or a missing calculation. Use source hashes to identify versions; never update a hash alone to conceal an altered premise.

For a completed candidate, save the prose/diff, impact record, specialist outputs and executed relevant checks. A substantial ontology-assisted review keeps its graph, manifest and query output. State the review's coverage and which conclusions remain conditional. Do not demand zero inherited open questions; new supported contradictions and affected earlier issues need a disposition.

For adoption, reconcile actual passages and current approvals, update character prose/registry together where needed, run required synchronization/validators and review the combined result. Author approval is distinct from technical success. Publication, integration and merging are separate scopes; use authorization already given without adding a redundant checkpoint.

For an automated round, record the agreed topic, endpoint, resource/iteration limits when supplied, execution status and a stopping reason. Save enough state to resume without redoing completed work or discarding author steering. Scheduling future rounds or messaging other user-owned chats requires the relevant user request; a procedure file alone does not create either service.
