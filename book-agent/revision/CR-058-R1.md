# CR-058-R1 — Doheny among the Five

**Status:** APPROVED and integrated on 6 November 2026 (branch `adopt/cr058-r1`), local labels retained. Self-reviewed: a small amendment to CR-058, checked with the canon linter, the character validator and the ledger; no separate fresh-context review.

**Authorization:** Author, 6 November 2026: "When does Logan Turner retire in canon? Does it make sense to bring Doheny into the fold of the five at that time? I think it's hard to keep him out when he wins so much." Then, on the proposal (DH-5): "I approve - go ahead and merge it as proposed."

**Read with:** CR-058 (NA-4, DH-1 to DH-4, CT10), CR-027 B6 (Doheny proposed as Turner's oil successor), CR-037 and CR-038 (the rules against capture; retirement at sixty-eight for estate heads except the Truce's named holders).

## What canon said

- Logan Turner (CT-LOGAN-TURNER) is born about 1838–1840 (facts file: 1839); his later fate is unresolved and no retirement year is stated.
- CR-038: every estate head retires at sixty-eight except the Truce's named holders (Silas, Kiyoshi, Soberanes). Turner is not one, so his seat falls vacant about 1907, the year of the Act of Settlement.
- CR-027 B6 proposes Doheny as Turner's later oil successor; CR-058 DH-4 seats Doheny in the Senate as the independents' voice (CT10, open).

## Decision (DH-5, APPROVED)

| Item | Decision |
| --- | --- |
| The seat | About 1907, when Turner reaches sixty-eight, Doheny takes Turner's seat among the Five (naval construction, heavy industry and the champions), where fuel policy now lives, and holds it until he reaches sixty-eight in 1924, the year Ryan replaces Tesla. |
| The Senate | DH-4's Senate seat runs from about 1896 to about 1907; his decade as the trust's critic is what earns him the seat. The Five bring the critic inside. |
| His companies | Under CR-037's rules against capture he keeps ownership and gives up management, to Canfield until Ned is old enough; the golden share keeps Pan American Pacifican; Ned takes over in the 1930s (DH-3). |
| Article 27 (1917) | Doheny of the Five must enforce the Realm's refusal of intervention on his own company, and does, furious. |
| Ireland (1919–1921) | The money comes from one of the Five while Britain is in the coalition: still private, but it costs him with Victoria. |
| Lago (1925) | Thalassia's refusal (PROPOSED in CR-058) falls the year after he leaves the seat. |

This settles CR-058 CT10 and CR-027 B6's Doheny proposal (approved). Open: Doheny's successor in 1924; Turner's life after the seat.
