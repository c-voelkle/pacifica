## What canon says now

- Elias is born about 1824 and grows up "in the southern Rocky Mountain trading world, around Taos"; birthplace and the family's arrival are OPEN, with "a Missouri birth followed by childhood migration" named as a possible route (§1).
- His father works a precarious holding and dies before the 1837 dispossession; the character bible names him **Silas Thorne** (CT-THORNE-FATHER) and leaves his life and death open. The mother is unnamed (CT-THORNE-MOTHER).
- The Colonel is a French émigré of Napoleon's military world, rank disputed, with Vauban, Jomini, engineering texts and Plutarch's *Lives*; name, service, rank and "eventual relationship to Elias" are OPEN (§1; unresolved item 1).
- A pastor strikes Elias over fossils and Genesis and Elias breaks his nose; locality OPEN. The pastor is the Rev. Silas Merritt, who takes in the widow and son (CR-022).
- The holding, creditors, jurisdiction and the cause of the fire in 1837 are OPEN.
- Kit Carson is not in canon. CR-061 (draft) brings him in for 1862–65.

## What this CR does

It settles Elias's childhood as a Missouri boy raised at the Missouri end of the Santa Fe Trail who winters at its Taos end; gives the father a life, a trade and a death; gives the Colonel a past, a place, a curriculum and an end; settles the one book Elias carries out of the fire; and threads Kit Carson through it, from the 1826 caravan to Taos in December 1862. The battle in which Carson's volunteers meet Elias's men is CR-061's; this CR adds what the two men carry into it and the bargain that brings Carson's companies over.
