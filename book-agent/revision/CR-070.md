## What this CR does

1. **It governs Alaska from the first day.** A Northern Commission brings the Mining Law of 1849, an assay office, claims courts, a land office and a survey that starts from the Russian American Company's own papers. Northern Alaska and Hokkaido become one school.
2. **It writes the reward into compacts.** A mine pays the nation that shows it the ore a share negotiated with that nation, on top of the state's 5%. Juneau's gold is worked from 1872, eight years early. The Seward Peninsula's placers are worked from 1876, followed up from the telegraph survey's notes.
3. **It makes the first years cheap.** From 1873 the garrison falls to 40% of its first strength, and its soldiers stay as settlers. Sitka is a station, not a fortress.
4. **It takes the north's rents.** The seal lease is re-tendered at a price per skin, and the herd is saved by a conservation regime proposed in 1884. A salmon case tax runs from 1870, with the nations' fisheries reserved by compact.
5. **It proposes the Bering telegraph.** It would give San Francisco a wire to Russia and Japan that touches no British cable. The line is OPEN pending research (author, 14 November 2026).
6. **It makes the road to the Yukon a compact.** The Chilkoot and Chilkat clans keep the passes and build the road with the Commission, so the route is ready before the Klondike.
