## D0. The claim in four sentences

1. Japan's abolition of the domains is not something Elias reads about. Pacifica's ally does it within months of the alliance, with men he has known since the 1850s, and its makers spend the following winter on Pacifican soil.
2. What he takes from it is a **sequence**, not an idea: he already wants the states gone (Part B). Japan shows the order of the steps and what each one costs.
3. He also takes a **warning**, from 1877: the one prefecture Tokyo left to its own men is the one that rose.
4. He applies the sequence well and the warning badly. In 1882 he agrees to keep the officers who fail Sherman's board together in one command (Part E, E1, E9.1); in 1887 the Truce of Thalassia gives that command to Silas for life, and Donner Pass follows.

## D2. The traffic runs west first (1867–1871)

- **D2.1 Kido reads the Articles of 1856. APPROVED.** Through Hikozō at Nagasaki, Kido and Itō get a first-hand account of a confederation whose members keep residual powers, their own militia and a veto. They conclude that Japan cannot afford one. The return of the registers is in part an answer to Pacifica's own constitution.
- **D2.2 The guarantee at Edo (canon, 1868)** puts Pacifican officers beside Katsu and Saigō at the moment the winners decide what to do with the losers' domains.
- **D2.3 The alliance of 1870–71 (canon) includes foreign credit. APPROVED:** part of that credit is what lets Tokyo promise to take the domain debts and the paper money. Elias is an underwriter of the abolition, not a spectator, and has the accounts.
- **D2.4 The guard.** Canon's army mission follows France's defeat. PROPOSED: its first officers arrive in 1871 and see the guard assembled.

*Effect:* "watched closely" (CR-021) becomes "helped pay for, and was told in advance".

## D4. The winter of 1872

In this timeline San Francisco is not a port of transit. It is the ally's chief city, and Pacifica is the one power with which Japan already has an equal treaty.

- **D4.1 A longer stay. APPROVED.** The mission spends six to eight weeks in Pacifica, from mid-January into March 1872: Carquinez and the yards, the Comstock, the irrigation works, Monterey. The treaty of 1870–71 is the model it carries on to Washington and Europe.
- **D4.2 Monterey. APPROVED.** Elias receives Iwakura, and talks at length with Ōkubo and Kido, on different steps. Kido can explain step one (change the title, leave the man). Ōkubo can explain step two (the guard, the morning and afternoon audiences, the bill). Elias can tell them what seven years of county grants have done to his own states. Each side is describing work in progress.
- **D4.3 Salt Lake. APPROVED:** the mission sees Deseret. It is invited unless the route east strands it there as historically; canon has not fixed the route. The men who abolished the domains five months earlier spend time in a member with its own militia, its own law of marriage and property, and a leader who is also the head of its church. What they say about it to their Pacifican escort, and what the escort reports to Monterey, is the scene.
- **D4.4 What is not said.** Elias does not announce an intention. In 1872 his members can still read newspapers.

## D6. The warning of 1877, and what Elias does with it — APPROVED

**What he sees.** His oldest Japanese friendships are in Satsuma (§7, §14). In 1877 Pacifica backs Tokyo against them (canon). Saigō, with whom Pacifica's officers stood at Edo in 1868, dies at Shiroyama fighting the state he helped found.

**The design consequence for the historic lands.** Kagoshima kept its own governor, its own revenue and its own armed men. California therefore keeps a language, a land law and a name, and nothing else: no officers of its own, no revenue of its own, no force. This is what canon already says ("historic lands with entrenched guarantees, not as veto players"); D6 supplies the reason.

**Hawaii is the exception (K-4, APPROVED; added 16 November).** It keeps a reigning Queen, the Queen's Guard as Thalassia's guard (E-10), the Territorial Council until 1907, eight aliʻi seats in the Senate (G2) and the crown's land premiums (CR-021; CR-057 E). By the rule of D6 it should be the most dangerous unit in the Realm. It is safe only because its Queen is the Regent: the one historic land with a head, a household guard and revenue of its own is the one whose head governs the whole. That sharpens the Kagoshima point. An exception is safe only while its holder is the centre, and the Truce makes one exception whose holder is not.

**Where he fails to apply it.** The failure has two dates (K-14, APPROVED; aligned with E-13).
- **1882.** At Sherman's request, and with Elias's consent, the officers who fail the Militia Act's examining board are kept together as a command of their own, the inland command (Part E, E1, E9.1). The founder gathers the men with a grievance into one body by an honourable act.
- **1887.** The Truce of Thalassia writes that command into organic law as the inland command, held for life by Silas (canon). That is a Kagoshima: a command that maps onto the old interior, held by a man with a following among the officers the examinations passed over (CR-022). Donner Pass, in October 1887, is Pacifica's Satsuma in miniature, and its leader is the founder's eldest son.

**The Chronicler (decision 10, APPROVED):** he does not draw the parallel between Saigō and Silas. The two scenes are set so that the reader sees it.

## D7. Where the model does not fit

- **Direction.** Japan merged about 300 units into 72. Pacifica dissolves a handful of members and, beneath them, merges roughly 200 inherited counties into 124. It is doing both at once.
- **Authority.** Tokyo had an emperor's rescript and a guard. Elias has two chambers and member legislatures. He must buy votes (canon), and the purchase leaves creditors: senators who remember what they were paid. See D9.
- **Legitimacy of the losers.** A lord's claim was hereditary and could be commuted. A legislature's is elective and cannot. Part H has to answer this.
- **Timing.** Japan did it in three years, at the start, while the winners were united. Elias takes sixteen and does it in his last year. Why he waits needs an answer: the hollowing has to come first; Hawaii (1874) and the Philippines take the 1870s; and until the succession presses, the grants give him most of what he wants at no political cost. With L1 approved, the wait is shorter than it looks: step one comes in 1879, and 1887 is step two.
- **The other models.** France in 1790 (canon) gives the rule for size. John Wesley Powell's report of 1878 on the arid lands gives the rule for lines: units drawn by watershed. Both belong to Part I.

## D8. What goes back to Japan

One line for now. Japan's own county, city and prefecture laws date from 1878 to 1890. Whether Pacifica's county of 1887, with its elected board and appointed Commissioner, leaves a mark on them is OPEN.

## D9. The oligarchy problem (opened 15 November 2026)

Japan's abolition was carried by a small band from two domains who had fought a war together and could act by rescript. Pacifica's leadership is one man and a coalition of unlike interests (Californios, San Francisco money, Mormons, Hawaiians, officers, the Japanese connection) working through elected chambers and member legislatures. The cohesion Satchō had by birth has to be manufactured. The author reopened the question of a political party as the instrument for this; it is developed in the sister CR-072 (the Prosperity Party), with its scope by 1887, its relation to the examination estates and its fate under the regency.
