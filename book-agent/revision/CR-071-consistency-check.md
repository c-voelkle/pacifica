## B. Tensions that need a sentence or a decision

1. **The handover and CR-022.** F–G's canon check (R) did not cover CR-022. B3 and B4 have Soberanes summoned to the deathbed in August and citing Elias's "last instruction" at Donner. Under G-11 Elias keeps no powers "so that no one can appeal from the Regent to him"; Soberanes does just that. It can stand, and it is better drama, but it should be listed under CT4. Add §22's "the founder's widowhood" as well: on 1 August Victoria becomes Regent as his wife.
2. **The San Francisco houses.** They subscribe to the Party from the winter of 1875 (CR-073 E2; CR-072 C9). Yet the Committee party holds California's legislature until November 1886, refuses the Collar in 1879 and carries the Port Hamilton resolution in 1885, and E-15 has Ralston bring the houses over only in 1886–87. One sentence must say what the money of 1875 bought and what it did not, or E-15 has nothing left to do.
3. **Ralston's guards.** E-15 and F3 put the chairman of the Patrimony into a legislative campaign. CR-073 E3 gives him published accounts, recusal and an independent board. E-15 is also not in CR-073, which goes in first, so CR-071 must carry the registry line.
4. **Where it nearly fails.** CR-072 (C7, table F) says Taylor's resistance. F–G says Deseret's consent is not legally needed, and the vote that is needed and narrow is California's. Decide for H1.
5. **The members.** F1 and F2 say "seven member legislatures"; no document lists them. Washoe, a member from 1867 with a Council delegation (CR-073 R23, R24), appears in no table of E2, E8 or G2. The outline dates the accessions 1862–65, but Columbia's charter is 1866 (G2) and Washoe's 1867. Outline CT6 (the Council after 1865) is still open, and the two-thirds of April 1887 rests on it; so does the question of who has chosen Hawaii's delegation since 1876.
6. **"Life seats"** (L6, G2, G4, Pacheco) against CR-038's retirement of every senator at 68, which G3 itself cites. Also "those of 1879": the men who took the commission then, or whoever holds the office in 1887?
7. **The first boards** are elected in early 1888 and the Franchise Law is of 1888. On which franchise?
8. **G4:** "many" of the 124 Commissioners are the treasurers and auditors of 1879. Five or six members with two posts each give a few dozen men at most. "Some of the first."
9. **E9.1:** "a Japanese admiral" in June 1884. Kiyoshi is 25 and takes the Kuroda name only in 1886.
10. **D0.4 and D6** date Elias's misapplied lesson to the Truce of 1887. E-13 moves it to 1882, with his consent. Align the two.
