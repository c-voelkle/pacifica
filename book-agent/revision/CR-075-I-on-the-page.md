# CR-075, Part I — On the page: the beats this CR adds (1858–1867)

**Status:** DRAFT, 18 November 2026. Everything here is PROPOSED except where it cites an approved part or canon. This part opens questions I-1 to I-6 (below).
**Belongs to:** `CR-075-outline.md`, Part I. It replaces the outline's three items I1–I3 (the July scene, the Chronicler's verdict, Lincoln's reading), which are folded in below. It uses CR-068's beat-sheet form (CR-068 Part F: beat, scale and pace, eyes, rounds and parts) so the beats can be placed in its sequence. "Eyes" is whose confidence the reader shares (CR-068 TL4).
**Sources:** Parts C (`CR-075-C-aims.md`), D (`CR-075-D-plan-and-people.md`), E (`CR-075-E-california.md`), F (`CR-075-F-hawaii.md`) and G (`CR-075-G-aims-to-peace.md`); CR-068's beats B3–B17.

## I0. What the beats are for

The author's brief (13:20): the war is not a one-man or a two-man show, and the answer to "how does he know?" is given alongside the plan. So the plan of 1861 is seen almost entirely through other people's eyes: Vallejo's, Victoria's, Sherman's, Baker's, Pico's, de la Guerra's and Lincoln's. Elias's eyes are used once, for the moment he watches someone else win the argument for him. The reader learns what he knows by watching people bring it to him.

## I2. What stays off the page

- **Starr King** has no scene of his own (E-10). He is heard through printed sermons on desks (I-B9) and through what others say.
- **The Republic's further purpose in the Pacific** is never stated, by anyone, in any beat. It shows only as some rocks, a clause about stations and a sealed minute (C, author's note of 19:47).
- **The contents of the sealed minutes** are not shown before I-B13. Baker, Lincoln and the Council's voters see only the preamble.
- **What Victoria answers in the rain** (I-B2) is not reported.
- **Kiyoshi** is not mentioned in I-B2.
- **The schoolbook formula** (Part H) is deferred and is not written into any beat.
