Scope: `storyline.diff` (every hunk), `records.diff` (register, bible, ledger), checked against `CR-081-outline.md` (A8, B10, C2a, C9, C8, C10, Canon tensions, Fact changes / impact notes), `AGENTS.md` and `authority-and-scope.md`. ## 1. Fidelity

**1. should fix — invented fact (§27, November 1908)**
- Text: "Peking says Guangxu was poisoned. No one establishes who gave the poison; …"
- Problem: neither the CR nor history has Peking announce a poisoning. C2a P-4c says only that the poisoning stays unproved, that "the suspicion of 1908 falls on them", and that "no character establishes who gave the arsenic, and the narrator does not say". An official statement is a new fact, and it is not what the court said.
- Fix: "At court it is whispered that Guangxu was poisoned. No one establishes who gave the poison; …"

**2. should fix — wrong attribution of tungsten (§26; also register requirements)**
- Text: "Hunan's Huachang company mines and refines antimony and tungsten from 1908 and sells to every buyer."
- Source: A2: Huachang (Wah Chang) is Hunan's antimony company with its Changsha refinery (1908); "Jiangxi's tungsten from the 1910s … dug by small miners and taxed by the province". A8.3 approves Chinese-owned mining and refining of both, with K. C. Li as the trader.
- Fix: "From 1908 Hunan's Huachang company refines antimony at Changsha, and from the 1910s Jiangxi's small miners dig tungsten; both are Chinese-owned and sold to every buyer." Change the register's "Huachang's tungsten and antimony from 1908" to match.

**3. should fix — approved decisions missing from the synopsis**
- P-7 (C9 lists "the southern delegation (1919)"; P-9: "The north–south peace conference at Shanghai, with Tang leading the south … sits in the same months"). The storyline does not have it, but the bible has it ("head of the southern delegation at Shanghai in 1919"). Fix, §30 after the Hague delegation sentence: "In the same months Tang Shaoyi leads the south at the north–south peace conference at Shanghai, which he leaves in disgust with both sides' generals."
- P-25: "From his resignation in 1926 no new works contract is signed with Mukden, and the existing ones run on." Missing from the storyline, though the bible has it (CT-WANG-YONGJIANG). Fix, §33 after "though not in its own mines and arsenal": "When Wang resigns in 1926 as war spending runs out of control, no new works contract is signed with Mukden, and the existing ones run on."
- C8, the Huludao exception: "The Huludao railway's receipts stay distinct from the customs-backed railway debt, because its loan has its own security". Without this sentence §25 contradicts §27 (see 8).
- **minor:** P-3, "the 1905 league still founded in Tokyo". The storyline names "the Tongmenghui's anti-Manchu wing" (§27) without introducing the league. Add to §26: "In 1905 the revolutionaries' league, the Tongmenghui, is founded in Tokyo, where China's students are."
- **minor:** C8 Korea, "*Decided:* a Chinese line with Japanese transit rights" for the Yalu crossing, and Korea's rice by contract on Japanese credit. Both are absent.
- **minor:** P-28, the Nanjing incident settled with Japan on 2 May 1929 and the embargo lapsing on 26 April 1929. P-29, the schedule expiring in May 1933 with no promised renewal. P-4e, "no Pacifican camera" at the funeral.

## 2. Consistency with the rest of the storyline

**7. should fix — Fong's plan contradicted (§33)**

## Plausibility concerns (matters CR-081 decided; noted only)

1. Kaʻiulani's 1908: she travels "from 1908" with Julian's European missions (spring 1908), is at Peking for Cixi's birthday on 3 November 1908 while Julian is still in Europe, and winters at Peking every year from 1902, all with the damaged valve that keeps her seated at The Hague in 1919.

## Summary

The integration covers nearly all of CR-081. Every P-item in C9, A8's material decisions and most of B10 appear as narrative. The Khingan name and the accession clause keep their PROPOSED labels, coverage comments are in place, older labels are restored after every insert, and the ledger quotes match the storyline. The serious defect is label scope. Trailing **[MODEL — CR-081]** labels appear in about a dozen places. They leave the figures under APPROVED and put approved decisions under MODEL: the reading of British traffic from 1925, the whole 1927 split and Mao's choice, the Jinan and Concord sequence, Formosa's council and nearly every social decision in §34. This must be fixed before merge (14). So must the missing registration of Ting Kwei Tang (26). Should-fix items:
- Peking's invented announcement of poisoning (1).
- Huachang's tungsten (2).
- Three approved details missing: Tang in 1919, Wang's 1926 halt, and the Huludao exception that §25 now contradicts (3, 8).
- The 1929 "first Chinese commissioners" clause, which undoes Fong's plan (7).
- The Hart relabel and the duplicated PROPOSED and APPROVED statements (9, 15, 16).
- The Harbin and Jinan placements and the stranded §33 summary (22–24).
- The memo phrasing (19) and long sentences (21).
- The ledger's parties and missing loan entries (27, 28).

The rest is minor.
