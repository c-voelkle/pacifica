## New passages

1. **CR-083, lines 196, 245, 256, 571; calendar, decision G. Caledonia is proclaimed without a signed guarantee.** Line 196 has the Association Treaty "voted before the Republic exists so that Caledonia is never without it", and the author's words on G are that it "ensures Caledonia is never unguaranteed". Line 245 says "The treaty holds no defence clause. The guarantee ... [is] the Concord's instrument". From about 18 July to the signing there is no guarantee in force or signed, only the delegations' vote of 9 to 11 July. Fix: either have the guarantee signed on or before Proclamation Day, or say in `A7` and `A10a` that for ten days the Republic stands on the delegations' vote alone, and tell the author that G does not do what his words say it does.
2. **CR-085, lines 170, 122, 125, 135, 139. Tate's cap arrives after the text is fixed.** Line 170: the cap on the Realm's share and the scaling down of the common budget are "Tate's. He argues in the Senate ... and he carries it", which is 9 to 11 July. Line 125: a week is enough "only because nothing in the text has changed since 15 June". Line 122: the Privy Council's advice is on "the quota's default, which is what the second condition became at Hayama". Fix: say how it enters, either as a condition of the Senate's consent that Tokyo accepts by cable before 16 July, or as a clause Julian carried to Hayama on Tate's demand. 3. **CR-085, lines 124, 168, 277; CR-086, lines 335, 347, 348.
