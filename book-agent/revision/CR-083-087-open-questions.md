## A. Worth deciding now (the 1932 chapters lean on them)

1. **Devices assembled after 1927** (CR-086). By 1932 the Works has material for some fifteen devices (MODEL); in 1927 three existed and one was spent. *Recommendation:* none is assembled after 1927. The material is kept as metal in the sealed store, and the two devices of 1927 stay assembled under the two keys. This makes the Realm's pledge credible without a count the reader must take on trust.
2. **The cipher room and the dominions' traffic at Fanning** (CR-084, T-B5c). *Recommendation:* it does not read it. CR-077's rule that a partner is never read is extended in 1932 to the dominions as treaty partners, and the station is worked by the cable company on its lease. This closes the item without a new secret.
3. **The meridian of the tenth-parallel protocol** (CR-084). *Recommendation:* 155° W, as the text has it. French Oceania's western edge lies at about 154°42′ W, so 155° keeps it outside, which is the point of the line.
4. **The form in which Pacifica holds the former German Samoa, New Guinea with Rabaul, and Nauru** (CR-084, T-P1). *Recommendation:* trusts of Ka Lōkahi, as Micronesia is. Nauru's phosphate is worked under the trust by a commission, with Australia and New Zealand buying at a published price (what Canberra was given in 1919). What stands on 1 January 1932: a wireless and coaling station at Apia, an administrator's post and a harbour at Rabaul, and the phosphate works at Nauru.
5. **The Union Senate's vote on the Final Act** (CR-086). The Union's signature is unratified on 1 August and the matter rides on the election of November. *Recommendation:* the Senate votes in 1933, under Young, after the book ends. The text then needs no margin.
6. **The Manila cable's landing** (CR-085). *Recommendation:* Woosung (Wusong), at the mouth of the Huangpu where it meets the Yangtze. The Great Northern and Eastern Extension cables landed there historically (*Hist.*, to be sourced).
7. **The yen's exact reset** (CR-085). The workbook uses 20% (Story) and 25% (Stress), inside the agreed band. *Recommendation:* 20%, as MODEL. The storyline's "a quarter to a third more in yen" on the last rescue instalments then reads "about a quarter".
8. **The percentages of the vote of 16 April 1932** (CR-083). *Recommendation:* about 58% for the terms on the mainland and about 44% on the Island, about 55% overall (MODEL). That is a clear win that still leaves the Island a grievance worth a bargain.
