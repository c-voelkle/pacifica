# What is left after the fifth reviews: a proposal (CR-083 to CR-087)

**Status:** PROPOSED by the drafter, 24 November 2026, at the author's request of 10:21. A working paper, not a CR. Nothing here is approved, and no draft has been changed to match it. The calendar findings are closed already (`CR-083-086-calendar.md`, approved at 10:10).

Each numbered item is a decision for the author. The reviews' numbers show what a "yes" closes. Part B needs no decision.

## A. Decisions

**1. Buchan in February (`CR-083-review-7.md` B1, S2, S8).**
Pattullo tells him before the ministry writes to Thalassia. Buchan answers in writing that he will sign and forward no request for union under another crown without a vote of the people on a published text, and he reports to the Dominions Office. The Senate's refusal of the fourth crown spares both men the test. His answer of 13 May then stands: Pattullo has broken no law, and Buchan knows it because he was told.
- His count is corrected: the Island asks him three things (a dissolution, reservation of the bills, separate treatment) and the press a fourth; he refuses three himself and forwards one, which London refuses.
- His set piece is the hand-over on Proclamation Day: the man who writes about the Empire for a living gives up Government House correctly, to a premier whose treaty the Realm's Senate consented to a week before.

**2. Buchan's appointment (S1).**
He is named at the end of 1930 and sworn early in 1931, the first Governor at Victoria chosen on the dominion's own advice, after the Imperial Conference of that autumn (*Hist.*, from memory; to be sourced). His death in 1940 goes into the registry entry.

**3. The franchise in Caledonia (S4).**
Everyone resident on Proclamation Day becomes a citizen of the Republic that day unless he declines within two years, which is the same term the Island has for keeping British nationality. The vote goes with citizenship. A Japanese subject who declines keeps the other floors (licences, the professions, title to land, the courts) and the right of entry and residence that Japan's clause gives. Pattullo's answer on the hustings, that the clause covers residence and not the vote, is then accurate. Vancouver gets one account of its split: labour fights the clause and the floors together, and the equal-wage article divides it.
- This narrows the author's words of 22 November, 16:57, in one respect: the floors "in voting" reach every resident who does not decline citizenship, and not one who does.

**4. The quota's default (`CR-085-review-6.md` S1).**
The draft says which way the audit is expected to go: it lowers Japan's 35 per cent, which is why Tokyo asked for it. The yards' share and the pool's share are fixed in their own instruments to the first review and do not move with the quota. If a review fails, the default may lower what Japan pays; it may raise what the Realm pays above 65 per cent only by a vote of the Realm's chambers. Tate is given that argument in the Senate and wins it, his one gain in his last fight.

**5. The common Navy minister (S6).**
The minister of 1927 to 1932 stays unnamed and is listed as OPEN. He gets one act at Hayama: he lays the order consolidating the reserved squadron before the Council himself. His successor, named by Julian in July under the new protocol, is Kiyoshi's son, who held the western squadron's Japanese units loyal in May. The naval party's loss then has a face: the officer who saved the Concord presides over the end of his father's squadron. His given name is not in canon and stays OPEN.
- "The navy" in Tokyo's cabinet fight is replaced by the persons meant. Which court tries the naval officers of 15 May is left out of this CR.

**6. Why the line runs on the Lewis crest (`CR-087-review-2.md` B1, Q1 to Q4).**
The peace of 1865 said "the crest of the main range" from the start. The meridian was a county line the territorial legislature drew in 1867, further east than the treaty allowed, and it is withdrawn as a matter of course when the survey arrives in 1874; "gets something for it" is cut. The only contest is between the two crests. The Union's surveyor argues for the western one and loses about 90 square miles of trough, on the words of the Union's own treaty of 1855 and on which range is higher. He is a real loser, and the revanchist press of the 1890s is cut, since the Union lost little.
- "About ten miles" stays in the prose and the exact point stays OPEN.
- The killings on the Marias and the starving winter are not mentioned; a later CR decides whether they happen.

**7. The approval of 07:27, by name.**
Four rows now list by name what that approval is taken to cover: `CR-083` R-12, `CR-084` R-10, `CR-085` R-7 and `CR-086` F-14. The author's words of 07:27 and 08:22 named only three of those items. A "yes" here confirms the four rows as listed; a "no" to any row sends its items back to PROPOSED.

**8. The British committee (`CR-084-review-7.md` S6, S7, Q3).**
The committee of six decides on authority the cabinet gave it; the full cabinet is told the conclusion without the paper, so Thomas knows in May that there is a decision and in June what it is. Eyres-Monsell tells the Chiefs of Staff when the order to dismantle the plant goes out. Hoare speaks one line, that he has seen what Thalassia does with a file it holds and the terms will not be better in a year. Whether the cipher room reads the dominions' traffic at Fanning stays OPEN.

**9. "In peace" in Article II (`CR-086-review-6.md` S2, Q4).**
It means peace between the Concord and that signatory. The article would therefore have covered Belgium in 1919, an ally at peace with the Realm, and `D2` says so; the sentence that it would not have reached 1919 is cut. This keeps the approved tension `T-8`.

**10. Chiyo (`CR-085` S5).**
One sentence: she inferred from Takahashi's letters where Tokyo was drifting and said nothing to Julian, so that the offer would be Japan's own.

**11. Hirohito's reason (`CR-085` S10).**
One sentence, PROPOSED: the coup was made in his name, and a prerogative that officers can invoke against his government is one he prefers to have settled by treaty. The Tokyo cabinet room, with Takahashi, Saitō and Araki, becomes a set piece.

**12. Three things confirmed as they stand.**
- Giannini loses in three drafts in one July, and that is wanted.
- The annuity years are read as the draft reads them: the instalment of 15 December 1932 is cancelled, and Britain next pays on 15 December 1938.
- Vance's death stays undecided in these CRs.
