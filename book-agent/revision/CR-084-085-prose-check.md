# Before-and-after content check of the restyled sections (24 November 2026)

Method. The old text is commit `b0afc4d`, the new text commit `2ee3e00` (identical to the working tree). Each restyled section of CR-084.md (B2, B3, B4, C1) and CR-085.md (A7, B1, C1, the four named bullets of C2 with the Kansai sub-bullets, D1 to D4, and the 1906 and 1908–1921 calendar rows) was read side by side, sentence by sentence. Backticked citations, status labels and numerals were also counted per section by script and every mismatch traced. Reordering, split sentences, removed bold and dashes, plainer wording, and the two stated exceptions (the "preliminary accord initialled in April 1932"; "near the Yangtze's mouth") are not listed.

## Verdict

The differences below. No number, date, percentage, range, personal name or place name was altered, and no citation was lost or changed. There is one real change of labels (CR-084 C1, "Why the Dutch sign"), one label reworded out of existence (CR-085 C1 heading), three narrowed *Hist.* markers, and a number of small glosses, dropped qualifiers and resolved ambiguities. House style is met in all restyled sections: no exclamation marks, no em dashes, no "this draft" or "the drafter".

## CR-084

### B2

1. Scope of the reverter clause made definite. Old: "Britain permanently halts ... and accepts a peacetime capital-ship cap east of Ceylon (...), subject to Britain's **reverter clause**." New: "Both are subject to Britain's reverter clause." The old sentence could be read as covering the cap only.
2. Qualifiers dropped. Old: "extracts an explicit diplomatic reservation". New: "obtains a reservation".
3. Date dropped (it stands in the anchor bullet). Old: "the £2,113,000 in the Commons statement of 1929". New: "The £2,113,000 of the Commons statement".
4. Wording. Old: "giving Wellington a concrete settlement". New: "gives Wellington something definite".

### B3

1. Old: "After Britain's preliminary agreement publishes the terms (guaranteeing municipal self-government, British-subject nationality opt-in, and port employment)". New: "The preliminary accord publishes the terms: municipal self-government, the option of keeping British-subject nationality, and employment in the port." "guaranteeing" is gone, and "opt-in" has become "the option of keeping".
2. Restrictive became descriptive. Old: "Labuan's British-subject Chinese merchants largely vote No". New: "Labuan's Chinese merchants, who are British subjects, largely vote No". The new text says all of them are British subjects.
3. Old: "Malay and domiciled local voters". New: "Malay and other domiciled voters".
4. Modifier moved. Old: "a direct commercial harbour lease on Labuan's foreshore". New: "a commercial harbour lease directly on Labuan's foreshore". In the old text the lease was direct (from Britain); in the new the word qualifies the place.
5. Added characterisation. Old: "cutting Brunei into two halves—*"Brunei is Limbang and Limbang is Brunei"*, *Hist.*". New: "the saying is "Brunei is Limbang and Limbang is Brunei" (*Hist.*)". "the saying is" is new.
6. Miri. Old: "past-peak **Miri** oil field (1910, peak 15,211 bbl/d in 1929, *Hist.*)". New: "Miri dates from 1910 and passed its peak of 15,211 barrels a day in 1929". "dates from" is a gloss on a bare year; figures unchanged.
7. Ceiling attached to the share. Old: "will not drop its Gulf crude liftings below a stated share of its 1929–31 average, at posted prices never below the compact's floor and inside `L1096 (...)`'s ceiling". New: "The prices are posted prices, never below the compact's floor, and the share stays inside the ceiling of `L1096 (...)`". The old text did not say what stays inside the ceiling.
8. Cause dropped and a citation moved. Old: "Because Mariveles needs no Cortes vote for ordinary contracts, the **Philippine Cortes votes on its own crown budget line (`C-1932-MARIVELES-DOCK`) to enlarge Mariveles's dry dock** (`L1234`)". New: "Ordinary contracts at Mariveles need no vote of the Cortes. What the Philippine Cortes votes is a line of its own crown's budget, to enlarge the dry dock (`C-1932-MARIVELES-DOCK`; `L1234`)." The commitment id was on the budget line and is now on the dry dock with `L1234`.
9. Tone. Old: "Dutch stabilization". New: "the propping-up of the Dutch".
10. Small words dropped. Old: "Resident **T. F. Carey**", "posted-price commercial offtake", "adjacent North Borneo shipping". New: "Resident Carey", "to take Seria crude at posted prices", "shipping along North Borneo".
