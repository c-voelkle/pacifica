# Pacifica — Legacy Secondary Cast Index

**Status:** Historical reference for roles not yet mapped to a new canonical identity. **These characters have not been deleted.** Names, occupations and descriptions below are taken from the original 250-profile [character_bible.json](../character_bible.json), not yet rewritten in light of the approved novel.

For canonical roles and newly introduced figures, use the [character bible](character_bible.md) and [character registry](character_registry.json). For all 250 mappings, including provisional matches, see [legacy_character_crosswalk.json](legacy_character_crosswalk.json).

Many details in these entries are now inconsistent with the approved plot. The index is intended to prevent accidental loss of potentially valuable perspectives, **not** to assert that a scene or a historical characterization still occurs exactly as described. Each row can eventually be given a stable CT-ID, merged cautiously with a confirmed role, or retired after editorial review. No merging by surname alone.

## Reconciliation priorities

- **Early political roles:** The old 1852 accession/secession scene, 1853 Pierce emergency and foreign-gold plot have been replaced by the sovereign 1846 republic and 1856 Confederation.
- **Military antagonists:** Union and Confederate officers should have their own professional agency and historically plausible service records; do not retire every cameo for convenience.
- **Hawaiian and Japanese roles:** The fictional royal and naval circles have altered legal history; origin and service dates may require complete recalibration.
- **Institutional roles:** A character named Chancellor, Regent, or Marshal might refer to a different office after the 1856 constitutional reordering.
- **Labor and colonial voices:** Avoid deleting miners, artisans, prisoners, engineers, workers and insurgents simply because they were unnamed. These roles offer essential polyphonic counterperspectives to the Chronicler.
- **Chronological generations:** Older and younger Kalākaua, Kuroda, Hoshino, Vance, Shen, Takashima and Thorne entries should be mapped only after pedigree and chronological verification.

**Current unmapped legacy sketches:** 183 of 250. Mapped names have their own canonical or provisional CT-IDs and are absent from this index.
