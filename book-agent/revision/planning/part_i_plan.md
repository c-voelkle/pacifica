The 1855 Defense Law provides alternative irrigation and engineering service for pacifist sects. Religious toleration does not imply a universal right to plural marriage: domestic marriage law remains with members, subject to the later confederal floor against bans by race or origin.

The Mormon and Washoe outcome questions are closed in direction. Deseret accedes in late 1862–1863; Young surrenders John D. Lee for trial and execution in 1863. Accession terms and instruments remain partly proposed. Washoe is retaken in 1863–1864 and confirmed by the 1865 settlement, ratified in 1867. Part I must seed those choices without treating them as completed events before 1861.

The later Western line keeps Denver, the Front Range, Pikes Peak, Leadville and Plains Union; New Mexico and the San Luis Valley join Pacifica, together with the Great Basin, Idaho, western Montana gold country and southwest Wyoming. Fort Benton stays Union. The full line is in [the audit scope](../war_base_assumptions.md); exact surveys and Native agreements remain qualified.

The 1861 population of about 1.2 million remains a modeling assumption. Family recruitment, women’s assisted passage, bilingual institutions and technical employment explain potential growth, but do not validate that figure. The existing family-programme workbook is a separate scenario; count its migrants and descendants once.

Later constitutional consequences must be seeded accurately: Lot retains the Kuhina Nui; no personal countersignature belongs to Kaiulani. The 1864–1865 consolidation uses dissolution and election followed by an expressly ratified enabling law. The 1874 Hawaiian incorporation tests the guarantees, and the 1887 constitution precedes any potential Confederate-state admission. Exact franchise, assent, sunset, title and post-1874 office status remain open.
