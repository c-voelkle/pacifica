## CORE EXEMPLAR VOICES (THE QUALITY BAR)

### `FUSETSUGAKI` (Secret Dutch-to-Japanese Bakufu Intelligence Reports)
- **Historical Context:** Confidential *Oranda Fusetsugaki* compiled by the Dutch *Kapitan* at Dejima (Nagasaki) and translated by hereditary *Oranda Tsūji* interpreters for the *Rōjū* (Senior Council) in Edo.
- **Voice & Register:** Formal early-modern bureaucratic translation prose (*Bakufu*, *Kurofune*, *Bugyō*). Obsessed with gun calibers, steam tonnage, silver-to-gold arbitrage, and how the "Western Protectorate of Kariforunia" alters Edo's coastal defense calculus.
- **Length:** 120–260 words.

### `HYORYUKI` (Castaway Journals of Japanese Fishermen — *Hyōryūki*)
- **Historical Context:** Brush-diaries or oral testimonies of Japanese coastal sailors blown off course by the *Kuroshio* current, rescued by Pacifica ships and brought to Monterey or San Francisco.
- **Voice & Register:** Naive, hyper-observant, concrete, and deeply moving. Describes locomotives, telegraphs, and foreign ports through physical comparison to village life, haunted by the knowledge that returning home under *Sakoku* edicts means execution.
- **Length:** 100–300 words.

### `HAWAIIAN_MELE` (Traditional *Mele* Chants Layered with *Kaona*)
- **Historical Context:** Court chants composed in Honolulu or Lahaina, presented with original `'Ōlelo Hawaiʻi` lines, an English translation, and a modern scholar's gloss unpacking the *kaona* (concealed political and tragic double meaning).
- **Voice & Register:** Poetic, elemental, sensory—weaving wind names, ocean swells, volcanic fire, and *lehua* blossoms to mask razor-sharp commentary on the covenant between the Crown and the Protector.
- **Length:** 70–180 words.

### `NUPEPA_LETTER` (Letters to Hawaiian-Language Newspapers)
- **Historical Context:** Reader letters from dockworkers, harbor pilots, or taro farmers published in *Ka Nupepa Kuokoa* or *Ka Hoku o ka Pakipika*.
- **Voice & Register:** Literate, biblical-rhetorical cadence mixed with street-level observation of Pacifica gunboats, coal depots, and how the Protector is perceived by ordinary islanders.
- **Length:** 100–240 words.

### `CLIOMETRIC_DATASET` (21st/22nd-Century Econometric & Forensic History)
- **Historical Context:** Future academic monographs (2020s–2120s) using satellite LIDAR, forensic metallurgy, digitized shipping manifests, and demographic mortality models.
- **Voice & Register:** Dry, clinical, statistically precise (`p < 0.001`, inline data series). Creates tragic irony by measuring the multi-million-life demographic shockwaves of a single intimate promise or tactical order.
- **Length:** 80–220 words.
