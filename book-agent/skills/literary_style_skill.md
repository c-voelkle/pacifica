# MASTER LITERARY STYLE & ANTI-SLOP STANDARD (literary_style_skill.md)

This novel must achieve the highest standards of contemporary literary fiction (in the lineage of Hilary Mantel, Cormac McCarthy, and Joseph Conrad). 

## I. BANNED AI SLOP & CLICHÉS (ZERO TOLERANCE)
1. **Banned Adverbs & Modifiers:** Absolutely no *suddenly, gently, quietly, fiercely, slowly, inevitably, tension-filled, palpable, echoing silence, breathless anticipation*. Let the physical action and dialogue carry the weight.
2. **Banned Participial Tails:** No lazy descriptive endings (*"..., leaving him to wonder," "..., casting a long shadow," "..., making it hard to breathe"*). Every sentence must end with a definitive noun, verb, or period.
3. **Banned Psychological Cliches:** Characters do not "feel a wave of emotion," "catch their breath," "stare off into the middle distance," or "realize the gravity of the situation." Translate internal states into physical interactions with the immediate environment.
4. **Banned Punctuation:** No em-dashes (`—`) as stylistic connectors. Use periods, semicolons, or clause structures.

## II. THE BENCHMARK AESTHETIC: MATERIAL DENSITY & HISTORICAL IMMERSION
1. **The Law of Tangible Weight:** Every scene must be anchored in physical reality before any abstract idea or political maneuver is discussed. Describe the grease on a brass breech-block, the smell of damp wool and salt cod, the exact temperature of a dry gulch wind, or the grain of a ledger page.
2. **Sentence Architecture & Cadence:** 
   - Alternate stark, muscular declarative sentences with long, rhythmic, polysyndetic clauses (*"and... and..."*) when describing movement, terrain, or machinery.
   - Favor active Anglo-Saxon verbs and concrete nouns over Latinate abstractions.
3. **The Unnamed Center (Subtext):** When characters discuss high-stakes strategy, war, or money, their true motives—fear, obsession, or devastating devotion—must remain submerged beneath the surface of the dialogue like the bulk of an iceberg.
