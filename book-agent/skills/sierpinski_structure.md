# SIERPINSKI GASKET FRACTAL NARRATIVE SKILL
This novel is structured as a self-similar Sierpinski Gasket across four nested scales:
Macro-Novel (3 Acts) -> Meso-Movement (9 Movements) -> Chapter (27 Chapters) -> Scene/Paragraph Micro-Triad.

## THE CORE TRIAD & THE TRAGIC VOID
Every scale of the book replicates the same three vertices around a central tragic void:
- **Vertex 1 (MIND / THE LEDGER):** Technocracy, strategy, naval logistics, economy, and the grand design of a new civilization.
- **Vertex 2 (WORLD / THE CRUCIBLE):** The tactile, scented, physical reality of ships, strange cities, desert campaigns, and social friction.
- **Vertex 3 (HEART / THE COVENANT):** The foundational human bond—pure, tragic love (Eros, brotherhood in arms, friendship, filial piety, sisterhood, agape) and its dark shadows (jealousy, desperation, obsession, fanaticism) that drives characters to build or burn the world.
- **The Central Inverted Void (THE TRAGIC COST):** The devastating moral paradox at the center of the triangle: a love so pure and absolute that honoring it sets in motion the ruin of millions of lives and the irreversible transformation of history.

## SELF-SIMILAR EXECUTION AT EVERY SCALE
1. **Chapter-Scale Resonance (`[Macro.Meso.Micro]`):**
   - Digit 1 sets the **Macro Act Gravity** (1 = The Design, 2 = The Crucible, 3 = The Reckoning of the Covenant).
   - Digit 2 sets the **Meso Movement Sub-Register** (shifting the focus across types of human bonds and shadows: e.g., `[x.1.x]` = Brotherhood/Filial Duty, `[x.2.x]` = Obsession/Jealousy/Rivalry, `[x.3.x]` = Eros/Sovereign Tragedy).
   - Digit 3 sets the **Dominant Voice & Immediate Trajectory** of the chapter.
2. **Scene-Scale Fractal Law:** Every scene must contain all three vertices in miniature:
   - Ground the scene in **tactile sensory reality** (scent, weather, texture — Vertex 2) and **strategic/economic stakes** (Vertex 1), but ensure the **true turning point of the scene** hinges on a human attachment, loyalty, jealousy, or private devotion (Vertex 3).
   - Leave the **Central Void** (the human cost of that choice) unspoken in the final concrete image or line of dialogue.
