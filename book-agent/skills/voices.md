# VOICE MODULATION MATRIX (voices.md)
Use the assigned ACTIVE VOICE PROFILE to govern sentence architecture, focalization lens, and metaphor domain.

## VOICE A: THE GEOMETER (Vertex 1: Technocracy, Strategy, Sea & Ledger)
- **Focalization Lens:** The world perceived as a grand system of levers, sea lanes, ballistics, credit, and statecraft. Characters notice tonnage, distances, telegraph ciphers, interest rates, and structural load.
- **Sentence Architecture:** Crisp, architectural hypotaxis. High precision, dry understatement, cool appraisal of power.
- **Metaphor Domain:** Navigation, surveying, double-entry accounting, metallurgy, clockwork, hydrography, chess.
- **Dialogue Style:** Clipped, transactional, strategic. Every question is an audit or a trap.

## VOICE B: THE ALKALI & SALT REGISTER (Vertex 2: Tactile Earth, Strange Lands & Friction)
- **Focalization Lens:** The raw sensory intoxication and physical resistance of the material world—the scent of crushed lime-leaves, coal smoke, monsoon rain on teak decks, alkali dust, and foreign markets.
- **Sentence Architecture:** Muscular, sensory parataxis alternating with long, rhythmic clauses steeped in place, weather, and physical labor.
- **Metaphor Domain:** Geology, ocean currents, botany, ordnance, horsemanship, culinary/aromatic textures, anatomy.
- **Dialogue Style:** Vernacular-tinged, direct, spoken under physical strain, heat, or the noise of ports and camps.

## VOICE C: THE COVENANT & THE SHADOW (Vertex 3: Love, Brotherhood, Piety & Obsession)
- **Focalization Lens:** The human heart under unbearable historical pressure. Characters notice the microscopic details of the people they love or fear losing: a scar on a wrist, a change in breathing, an unspoken fatigue, an old debt of honor.
- **Sentence Architecture:** Quiet, high-tension restraint. Long, supple sentences that carry immense emotional weight beneath a composed surface, punctuated by short, devastatingly plain sentences where the mask slips for a fraction of a second.
- **ANTI-CLICHÉ RULES FOR WRITING LOVE & DEVOTION:**
  1. **Never Name the Emotion Directly:** Ban melodramatic declarations, lingering gazes, aching chests, and romance-novel tropes. Show love through *displacement*—through what a character remembers, protects, sacrifices, or destroys for the other person without ever asking for credit.
  2. **Honor the Specific Register of the Bond:**
     - *Eros (Elias & Kaiulani):* Intellectual and political equals whose intimacy is dangerous because every private tenderness between them alters the fate of nations.
     - *Philia / Brotherhood in Arms:* Expressed through dry humor, shared physical hardship, unspoken tactical cover, and fierce protective action.
     - *Storge / Filial Piety & Sisterhood:* Expressed through duty to lineage, memory of home, and the quiet terror of failing those who depend on you.
     - *The Shadow Registers (Jealousy, Obsession, Fanaticism):* Never write fanatics or jealous rivals as raving cartoons; write them with terrifying, lucid devotion to the thing they worship or refuse to lose.
