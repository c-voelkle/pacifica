# Pacifica power review

The [compact review ontology](ontology/README.md) represents dated claims, qualified control, evidence applications and comparisons for the gold-to-revenue assessment. It includes a Turtle pilot dataset, separate SKOS vocabularies, a small PROV-O profile, SHACL validation and SPARQL review queries.

The profile preserves the authority of the [master storyline](../../book-agent/revision/master_storyline.md) and approval register. It supplies an inspectable representation of a bounded review; it does not approve narrative changes or add a power simulation.

[Aegis](../../.agents/skills/aegis/SKILL.md), [Clio](../../.agents/skills/clio/SKILL.md) and [Chryseis](../../.agents/skills/chryseis/SKILL.md) share the [ontology handoff](../../.agents/skills/aegis/references/ontology-workflow.md). Chryseis is the maintained successor to Pacifica Scenario Finance; its financial references are retained. Full Aegis reviews produce a readable assessment plus a validated, separately scoped review graph.

The [matched-source before/after comparison](../../outputs/power-review-gold-ontology-20261026/comparison.md) includes the separate Aegis rerun, executed validation, financial parity and a carried-forward issue register.

[Aumakua](../../.agents/skills/aumakua/SKILL.md) reviews internal and cross-text consistency independently of the power objective: chronology, identities, institutional authority, knowledge, counts and quantities. It reads the prose before graph output or prior findings, distinguishes contradictions from open decisions and compatible readings, and records actual coverage. The ontology is an optional aid for this review; validation cannot establish that extraction captured every conflict. Use Aumakua for a continuity audit and Aegis to assess the power consequences of a proposed repair.

Its [first evaluation protocol](../../outputs/aumakua-pilot-20261026/protocol.md) covers a bounded Pacifica prose audit and a fresh fictional fixture, with earlier findings and the answer key withheld from the evaluators.
The [results](../../outputs/aumakua-pilot-20261026/results.md) record recovery of both omitted textual questions, six detected fixture conflicts and preserved compatible readings, with the reviews and execution limits archived.
