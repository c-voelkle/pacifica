# Collaborative narrative development

Mahoe, Kalai and Imi join Aegis, Clio, Chryseis and Aumakua as a working process for Pacifica. The intended rhythm is the author's recent development sessions: explore a concrete question, challenge a favored instrument, compare consequential alternatives, follow corrections, write when requested and integrate actual prose only within the authorized scope. The author can redirect the discussion or return to exploration at any point.

## Follow the author's intent

The session is not a fixed seven-stage pipeline. A question about control of a region might need historical evidence and a concrete institutional explanation. A strategic comparison may need several political branches and financial estimates. A narrow wording repair may need none of those. Mahoe coordinates only the contributions needed to answer the current question.

The current request determines the working mode:

- **Explore:** Develop and challenge possibilities; substantive analysis does not authorize CR or canon edits. “Still brainstorming” remains binding until the author changes that scope.
- **Compare:** Pin baseline/cases and evaluate mechanisms, costs and outcomes. Retain an option that preserves the baseline; a new premise requires new consequences.
- **Draft:** Produce a complete standalone proposal or actual candidate prose with knock-on effects and explicit unresolved choices. Drafting a CR does not adopt it.
- **Adopt:** Use the authorization actually given to revise the master and affected records, synchronize and validate. Do not repeatedly seek approval for already-authorized work. Publishing separate alternatives remains distinct from adoption or merging.

These are descriptions of intent, not a new executable state engine. A session can move between them. A correction to the objective may change a recommendation without authorizing a new plot event. A direct authorial correction or integration request authorizes its stated scope.

## Keep the objective and the people visible

The [power success contract](../../.agents/skills/aegis/references/success-contract.md) concerns effective capacity through 1932, with separate outcomes and explicit transmission, burdens, refusal and succession. A port, territory, institution, gross receipt or larger economy is not automatically success. Mahoe should reopen the choice of instrument when discussion becomes attached to obtaining one.

Earlier actors have their own purposes and contemporary information. Explore what a change alters and leaves intact, including outcomes if war is avoided or a partner refuses. Do not assume that the author's selected endpoint is inevitable or quantify narrative probabilities without defensible support. Clarifying an existing mechanism can improve explanatory support without increasing power in the world.

Kalai writes the selected narrative form. A continuous synopsis should describe decisions and consequences; it need not become a chapter with invented dialogue. Imi reviews that form, respecting the author's objective while asking whether the text earns its successes, preserves other actors' choices, differentiates perception and gives events narrative consequence. It does not impose a moral ending or make every capable protagonist fail.

## Preserve continuity through the work

For a substantial draft, record changed premises, affected passages and the downstream chain. Use distinct cases for alternatives. Pair graph dependency traversal with raw prose searches and reading: existing extraction is incomplete. Changed premises leave dependent assessments stale until reassessed; changing hashes alone does not resolve them.

Use the ontology for Claims, MechanismLinks, EvidenceApplications, QuantityClaims, comparisons and Aumakua findings. Use ordinary session records for workflow and author decisions. Literary preferences are not contradiction classes. The graph is not a simulation engine, complete impact map, narrative approval mechanism or substitute for reading.

For meaningful independent review, use bounded in-task specialist passes with matched source inputs and withhold advocacy during the initial assessment. Record independence honestly; repeated opinions do not supply historical corroboration. Subsequent reconciliation must retain earlier unresolved issues and explain resolutions. Individually successful patches need review together before integration.

## Completion and automation

A completed round provides a useful answer, supported comparison or actual revised prose, together with relevant evidence, calculations, coverage, checks and consequential author choices. Conditional results are valid. Continue useful work while a choice is pending; do not fabricate a premise to make the round appear complete or iterate until all inherited uncertainties disappear.

Mahoe's [session record reference](../../.agents/skills/mahoe/references/session-records.md) supports resumable work. These are installed skill procedures and specialist handoffs. They do not install a recurring scheduler, call Claude, message other user-owned chats, or create an executable orchestration service. Those actions need their own actual implementation and relevant authorization. Supplied external critiques can inform the session with their provenance retained.

The first [role evaluation](../../outputs/narrative-cycle-skills-20261026/results.md) uses isolated fictional material. It tests observable decisions and deliverables, not a guarantee of whole-book improvement or a comparison between model providers.
