# Collaborative narrative development

Mahoe, Kalai and Imi join Aegis, Clio, Chryseis and Aumakua as a working process for Pacifica. The intended rhythm is the author's recent development sessions: explore a concrete question, challenge a favored instrument, compare consequential alternatives, follow corrections, write when requested and integrate actual prose only within the authorized scope. The author can redirect the discussion or return to exploration at any point.

## One rule set, seven roles

All seven skills apply the [shared authority and scope rules](../../.agents/skills/mahoe/references/authority-and-scope.md):

- **R1** Authority of sources and local labels
- **R2** Working modes (Explore, Compare, Draft, Adopt) and what each authorizes
- **R3** No invention to close gaps; protected mysteries
- **R4** Contemporary actors and their information
- **R5** Review independence
- **R6** Sources, outputs, versions and relative paths
- **R7** Limits of the ontology, validators and skills

Each SKILL.md cites these rules by number and describes only how its own role applies them. Change a shared rule in that one file.

## Follow the author's intent

The session is not a fixed seven-stage pipeline. A question about control of a region might need historical evidence and a concrete institutional explanation. A strategic comparison may need several political branches and financial estimates. A narrow wording repair may need none of those. Mahoe coordinates only the contributions needed to answer the current question. The modes describe intent, not an executable state engine.

## Keep the objective and the people visible

The [power success contract](../../.agents/skills/aegis/references/success-contract.md) concerns effective capacity through 1932, with separate outcomes and explicit transmission, burdens, refusal and succession. A port, territory, institution, gross receipt or larger economy is not automatically success. Mahoe reopens the choice of instrument when discussion becomes attached to obtaining one.

Kalai writes the selected narrative form. A continuous synopsis describes decisions and consequences; it need not become a chapter with invented dialogue. Imi reviews that form, respecting the author's objective while asking whether the text earns its successes, preserves other actors' choices, differentiates perception and gives events narrative consequence. It does not impose a moral ending or make every capable protagonist fail.

## Session records

Substantial or resumable cycles keep a session record under `outputs/sessions/<YYYYMMDD>-<slug>/`, following Mahoe's [session-records.md](../../.agents/skills/mahoe/references/session-records.md) and its [template](../../.agents/skills/mahoe/references/session-record-template.md). The record holds the question, mode, authorization, pinned baseline, cases, specialist handoffs, the three separate review judgments, author decisions, open choices and the stop reason. The ontology keeps Claims, MechanismLinks, EvidenceApplications, QuantityClaims, comparisons and Aumakua findings; the session record links to them rather than duplicating them.
