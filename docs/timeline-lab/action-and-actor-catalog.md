# Pacifica action and actor catalog draft

**25 October 2026 · Design revision 0.10 · Draft for author review**

Pacifica chooses offers, budgets, investments and authorized commitments. The world model determines what resources become available, how other actors respond and what follows. Strategies can adapt to dated observations; the author does not need to describe the outcome of every branch.

This catalog proposes **24 action types and 25 actor roles or templates** for the 1904–1932 pilot and its later extensions. A template is a requirement to create distinct actors where their interests and powers differ, not a single agent representing all banks, Japanese officers or northern peoples. Activate only the actors and actions supported by the selected campaign.

Use the [objective and constraint sheet](objectives-and-constraints.md) for the scorecard and limits. The [structured draft catalog](draft-model-catalog.json) contains complete actor resources, observations, activation, evidence needs and action counterparty mappings. Each action starts disabled for search until its model coverage is usable. The [ontology](ontology.md) defines attempts, adjudication, information, intentions and qualified obligations. This is a draft contract, not a simulation implementation.

## Control and agency

Open proposals vary only declared actor-admissible policy/procedure choices; they do not force counterpart outcomes or erase personal commitments. Record plausibility, actual controller selection and isolated consequence evidence separately. An independently plausible author intervention may show conditional consequences and a different next dilemma under unchanged continuation rules, but never proves actor selection or reliability. R0 also demonstrates intention-sensitive generated choices and a separate low-effect contrast.

Each modeled choice has a DecisionContext identifying its person/collective decision-maker, represented institution or personal capacity, dated role/authority, resource owners, information snapshot and actual procedure. The control binding explains how Pacifica's policy interacts with selected personal commitments and independent institutional choices; optimization cannot silently erase those commitments. DecisionRecord retains inputs actually used, considered alternatives and reasons. Officeholder substitution preserves institutional liabilities under explicit private-state transfer rules; author overrides receive no retrospective motive.

R0 selects one financing/commitment dilemma from this broader catalog, including a few consequential beliefs/plans and one supported actor-scoped conduct comparison. The full 24-action/25-role roster is not required for the first slice. R1 adds assessed strategy and bounded narrative-condition search; the general editor, structural generation and optional LLM prose follow in R2.

The proposed control mask gives the optimizer Pacifica's executive agenda and authorized operational decisions. It may seek legislative approval, propose constitutional change or invite private investment. The Senate, Chamber, Hawaiian and Philippine institutions, banks, firms and affected communities retain the powers and choices appropriate to them. The exact domestic control granularity remains for author review.

Other states retain their own objectives, budgets, observations, institutions and alternatives. Japan may refuse a quota; China may withhold practical rail cooperation; a private lender may decline a bond. Receiving assistance does not automatically produce loyalty. Independent actors may cooperate or correlate without sharing a decision process.

Reserve Bank delegation and the division between government and monetary authority need a dated charter specification. The optimizer cannot spend all national gold or direct every domestic private bank by assumption.

Distinguish formation and survival from names. A Siberian government exists only if generated events establish it. A federal Russian partner and an independent Siberia have different powers and obligations. A common Concord command exists only after its competent parties create it; Japan's home army does not automatically become Pacifica's army.

For each active actor instance, register its stable identity, kind, legal name, jurisdiction, active dates, controlled resources, constraints, information and response model. Persons, offices, institutions and factions remain distinct. Dated role tenure and explicit succession rules preserve institutional assets and obligations. Reference existing character IDs or an explicit crosswalk rather than merging names. The detailed records in the JSON identify the evidence needed. Important cases include:

- **Domestic consent:** separate chambers, affected county blocs and the three internal crown institutions. Ka Lōkahi's three internal votes and ten-year matters differ from the Concord's two parties and five-year executive term.
- **Japan:** civilian government, Diet/court roles, distinct military factions, BOJ, banks and industry. An agreement with the cabinet cannot erase factional resistance or a currency gap.
- **China and transit:** legal ownership, central authority, regional control and practical railway management may lie with different bodies.
- **Russia and Siberia:** imperial/constitutional/provisional/Bolshevik governments and opposition movements have different incentives. Aid cannot select an election, a regime transition or Wrangel's victory.
- **Local rights:** instantiate affected Sakha, Buryat, Chukchi, Koryak, Aleut and other communities separately. A sovereign signature cannot represent every local interest.
- **European settlements:** July/Bosnia requires actual Serbian and other indispensable parties; general peace also requires the affected Italian/Ottoman and other participants. Agadir requires Morocco, Spain and existing rights to be represented. A pooled “Europe” actor cannot supply their consent.

Future political alternatives must be explicit branches, not an accidental officeholder substitution. ## Behavior and uncertainty

Start with interpretable rules: what minimum terms an actor can accept, what it can afford, what it observes and who can veto it. Test alternatives for bargaining, faction strength, response delay and institutional capacity. This catalog supplies no calibrated political acceptance probabilities.

Separate uncertainty in external conditions, unknown coefficients and alternative behavioral structures. Weather or external disruptions may be exogenous; war, bank runs, credit withdrawal and regime change can depend on policies and actor responses. Do not paste in a baseline crisis when its causes have been removed.

Selected institutional and personal goals, beliefs, persistent plans and promises can explain consequential behavior. Delivered observations update beliefs under registered rules; they do not automatically establish truth or agreement. Preserve abandoned and failed plans with their reasons. Unsupported psychology remains author interpretation.

Actor decisions read a frozen observation state before simultaneous choices resolve. Sequential negotiations use declared rounds and information updates. Opposition cannot react to Pacifica's hidden future order, and Pacifica cannot read an opponent's latent preferences. The same boundary applies to action menus, eligibility masks, previews, errors and automatic alternatives. Hidden actual feasibility is resolved after an observation-consistent attempt, not exposed by filtering the actor's options. Response search declares what policy commitments are public and whether replies are unilateral or jointly modeled.

Historical event calendars are appropriate for labelled replay or conditional tests. General strategy discovery needs varied external conditions and fresh assessment worlds, including sensitivity to event timing, so the optimizer cannot succeed merely by preparing for a secretly fixed historical date.

A language model may help draft reviewed assumptions and explain traces. Numerical adjudication follows registered, reproducible mechanisms; it does not invent a new loss rate or actor preference during a rollout.

## Action contract

### Relational repertoire required for R0

The positive chain must pass these counterexamples as well: hidden betrayal alone does not change an uninformed recipient's beliefs/choices; breaking a nonbinding personal promise does not automatically make conduct illegal; and a truthful disclosure need not generate belief, trust or cooperation. Evidence/inference channels, credibility rules and goals determine the response. Actual legality and promise fulfillment remain separate; legally permissible promise breaking can use lawful execution while retaining supported social consequences.

The 24 existing macro action families remain disabled design entries. M0 must define stable variant IDs and executable bindings for a bounded relational repertoire using the same action protocol:

| Proposed variant | Required semantics |
|---|---|
| Private commitment | Named parties, promised act/condition/deadline, acceptance/formation, visibility and resulting duty; a promise is not guaranteed performance. |
| Disclose or withhold | Information actually possessed, claimed content versus truth, channel/delivery time, audience and cost. Silence becomes evidence only through an explicit expectation rule. |
| Verify or challenge | Request, available evidence, independent responder and possible delay/refusal; verification cannot reveal an unmodeled or protected answer. |
| Honour or breach | Check an existing duty and actual conduct; apply only supported cooperation/relationship effects, with persistent state and future options. |

These variants reuse existing Commitment, Assertion, Observation, Belief, ActionAttempt and qualified relationship records; no universal personality model is required. Only reviewed, tested variants become active in the content manifest. Require two plots with similar financial results but distinct commitment/disclosure/belief/choice/cooperation chains. Responsibility for Noe's death stays protected and unresolved even when suspicion affects these choices.

### Existing action families

Every ActionAttempt requires an initiator, targets, decision time, actor observation snapshot, typed parameters, eligibility rule, selected profile, plan references and provenance. Its Adjudication separately records actual preconditions, legal status, counterpart replies, reservations, disposition, realized events and disclosure rules.

The lifecycle distinguishes proposed, negotiating, authorized, committed, implementing, effective and closed phases. Rejection, refusal, deferral, partial execution, failure, exit and cancellation are explicit dispositions. Legal status is separate from phase: a supported breach transition can execute without lawful authorization, while retaining unauthorized status and consequences. Some domestic actions omit negotiation; lawful execution still needs its required approval and implementation.

A proposal can incur time and cost without creating a loan, treaty or investment. Reuse the same financing and capacity references across bundled actions; do not create an extra capital stock or duplicate spending when credit funds construction. Amounts must be nonnegative and shares within zero to one, but affordability and acceptable terms also depend on law, resources and counterparties.

The listed preconditions describe **lawful execution for the bound actor/action family**. Every profile permits lawful execution. `apparent_authority` adds registered mistaken-authority execution; `breach_exploration` adds registered intentional breach to both earlier categories. Actual legality is independently adjudicated: valid authority produces a lawful result under any profile, and enabling breach never compels misconduct. Bind actors/families/dates and precedence explicitly; every exceptional variant needs separate enabling and consequence coverage. All actions/exceptional variants remain disabled. M0 defines the bounded comparisons. Physical/accounting invariants, locked premises and independent consent hold throughout.

Follow the [adjudication protocol](ontology.md#action-and-adjudication-protocol). Register resource priorities/ties, reservation release and atomic versus partial settlement for linked actions. Incidental actor/module order cannot allocate resources. Actual failure may follow a reasonable attempt without leaking hidden facts beforehand.

Every active mechanism declares runtime applicability, read/write ownership, evidence and intervention/counterexample tests. A failure propagates to dependent claims; an explicitly selected provisional continuation creates a linked conditional branch. Counterparty identities and full contracts remain in the JSON; intended effect links do not guarantee gains.

### Propose and execute a public budget

**ACTN-01 · fiscal**

- **Choose:** Tax/fee proposals within lawful instruments; Service and investment appropriations; Priority order; Contingency reserve.
- **Required:** Competent legislative approval for changes; existing duties, local/crown budgets and essential services mapped.
- **Resources and delay:** Revenue arrives on its collection schedule; services use labor/materials. Tax and spending changes affect households and firms with lags.
- **Independent response:** Amend/refuse appropriations; Change investment, employment or tax compliance.
- **Realized result:** Authorized taxes and payments; endogenous service, demand and capacity effects.
- **Failure checks:** Unfunded promises; Cutting essential services to inflate cash; Counting local receipts twice.
- **Links:** OBJ-01, OBJ-03, OBJ-06; CON-01, CON-04, CON-05, CON-08, CON-09, CON-10. ### Issue or restructure public debt

**ACTN-02 · fiscal**

- **Choose:** Face amount; Currency; Maturity and amortization; Coupon/price offer; Collateral and covenants; Tender/issue timing.
- **Required:** Legal authority, disclosed existing debt and feasible investor funding; refinancing cannot erase liabilities.

- **Realized result:** Actual subscribed debt and cash; creditor losses or maturity changes by agreement.
- **Failure checks:** Rollover failure; Gold/FX mismatch; Future debt hidden by terminal date.

### Offer public or syndicated credit

**ACTN-03 · credit**

- **Choose:** Named borrower and use; Public commitment; Private invitation; Currency/rate/maturity; Draw conditions; Collateral; Exposure cap.
- **Required:** Borrower exists and can legally accept; public appropriation and funding capacity; private participation remains voluntary.
- **Resources and delay:** Commitment reserves exposure; cash only at accepted draws. Administration, credit losses and debt service follow actual schedules.
- **Independent response:** Accept/reject/counteroffer; Private undersubscription; Draw partially; Repay, default or seek restructuring.
- **Realized result:** Separate public, private and foreign claims; borrower investment only after real resources are obtained.
- **Failure checks:** Treating the whole syndicate as Treasury property; Loan proceeds counted as GDP; Automatic political alignment.
- **Links:** OBJ-01, OBJ-03, OBJ-05, OBJ-08; CON-01, CON-04, CON-05, CON-10, CON-11, CON-18. ## Strategies the search can generate

A strategy links conditions observed at the time to action parameters, including timing, scale, stop rules and fallback. It may use a date for a genuine budget or treaty deadline; it cannot inspect future shocks. Begin with interpretable rules rather than arbitrary black-box commands.

| Seed | Conditional pattern | Intended comparison |
|---|---|---|
| POL-01 — Fiscal-first restraint | Review discretionary commitments when cash falls below the selected buffer. Prefer staged commitments and a declared exposure cap. Maintain selected minimum security and essential-service obligations. | Simple comparator, not the existing control fixture. |
| POL-02 — Productive partnership | Stage infrastructure, skills and credit around partner acceptance and usable capacity. Continue only when public funding, counterpart performance and transport remain adequate. Allow partners to choose competing offers and retain their own gains. | Starting policy family; search varies terms and thresholds within feasible bounds. |
| POL-03 — Early mediation with conversion support | Offer mediation when dated observations meet selected escalation indicators. Pair terms with bounded, credible civilian-conversion support. If parties refuse, reassess; peace is an output, never a policy parameter. | Starting policy family; search varies terms and thresholds within feasible bounds. |
| POL-04 — Limited security commitment | Commit only to declared missions with consent and sustainment. Tie reinforcement to actual bottleneck/stockpile conditions. Reassess or withdraw under specified lawful fallback triggers. | Starting policy family; search varies terms and thresholds within feasible bounds. |

Always include wait/review and simple policies compatible with the selected profile under the same evaluation budget. Compare optimized policies with these seeds and random search, then assess finalists on fresh worlds and alternative response models. Report the best-found trade-offs and their failure conditions. Keep an independent author archive for interesting dominated policies/histories. Narrative route or minimum-change search uses declared predicates, intervention fields, costs/order and budgets; targeted samples retain their selection denominator. Fresh unconditioned assessment is required for ordinary policy-success claims.

## Implementation boundaries for the pilot

Choose one coherent financing/commitment dilemma with the sustainable capability and political consequences its selected objectives require. Include a consequential choice, a selected belief/plan change and a supported actor-scoped conduct comparison; the wider finance/diplomacy/security catalog remains future scope. A global roster is unnecessary when the question is bounded; every indispensable party and material effect within that question is necessary.

Enable a family only when it has:

1. An initial state and native resource/accounting model.
2. Dated legal authority, ownership, consent and observation rules.
3. Independent counterpart responses, including refusal and an outside option.
4. Costs, delays, implementation and failure/exit consequences.
5. Evidence/assumptions and usable objective coverage.
6. Reproducible uncertainty, terminal treatment and fresh assessment cases.
7. Active ontology invariants, observation-safe eligibility/adjudication and declared resource arbitration.
8. Runtime applicability and supported failure/consequence behavior, including each separately enabled breach variant.

A family that lacks these remains unavailable or supports an explicitly selected narrower conditional experiment with a registered provisional mechanism. An imposed-event branch explores consequences without certifying that a policy generates the event. All branches retain complete decision state and their intervention labels. A six-case viewer alone does not deliver the discovery goal. See [requirements](requirements.md), particularly TL-041–057 and TL-068–076, the [ontology](ontology.md), and the [product vision](product-vision.md). The R0 author review of real writing questions precedes broader tooling and search-scale commitments.
