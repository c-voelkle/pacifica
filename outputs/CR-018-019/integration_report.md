# Pacifica integration of CR-018 and CR-019

**Updated:** 18 October 2026. **Order:** CR-018, then CR-019. **Status:** Master and current supporting records reconciled; source proposals and open recommendations remain qualified.

The [master storyline](../../book-agent/revision/master_storyline.md) now ends with the surviving, dominant Pacific order in 1932. Its Pacific and European arcs share a causal sequence: Japan’s growing strength creates the stakeholder compact and union; the coalition wins Siberia; Pacifican credit brokers the European peace; the depression brings the sovereign Union into the creditor order. Labor reforms and the hidden Altai record preserve the human cost of that victory.

The original attachments are preserved byte for byte as [CR-018](../CR-018/CR-018-original.md) and [CR-019](../CR-019/CR-019-original.md). Their full text and decision labels are also retained in the change register. CR-017 was already folded into CR-018 and was never separately integrated. The intermediate master after CR-018 (intermediate local snapshot; current storyline is published under book-agent/revision) and separate stage differences document the requested order. The pre-merge revision is backed up under `tmp/cr018019-before-integration`.

## Approval distinctions and remaining questions

CR-018’s 21 author-decision rows and CR-019’s 10 are preserved in the register. Numerical figures and PROPOSED execution do not become approved because the documents were merged.

- **OPEN-018-18:** the entire revised Vance III arc remains proposed: lineage through a private father, Soberanes’s patronage, near-foster-brother bond, exact logistics role, prisoner circumstances, confession and May 1932 death. CR-019’s archive recommendation does not silently approve that arc.
- **OPEN-018-20:** the 1905 peace signing place remains open; Vladivostok is the recommendation.
- **OPEN-019-03:** decided by the presidential sequence. The other twelve OPEN-019 recommendations remain open, including Hague venue/date and broker, Hoover’s role, British payment in kind, Dominions treaty, London raid, German crown, labor charter, archive, Pensacola, Roosevelt’s cabinet office and privacy of the 1917–1918 disagreement.
- **CR-016:** OPEN-016-05 and 08 are resolved; 01, 03, 04 and 07 remain. OPEN-016-06 depends on the still-proposed Vance arc. Source numbering remains intact.

Other proposed compact, union, war and treaty mechanics retain their labels. The working master presents those mechanics as a readable scenario with qualifications, rather than leaving incompatible plot variants active.

## Continuity qualifications

| Source tension | Treatment |
|---|---|
| CR-019 says Senate ratification in 1865 | Preserve the earlier explicitly approved 1865 peace framework and 1867 Western treaty ratification. The 1932 concession ends the surviving Southern claim. |
| CR-018 sometimes says Russia has no Pacific port or renounces its whole coast in 1905 | Its approved territorial sequence governs: the southern outlet is lost in 1899; Kamchatka, the Okhotsk coast and Nikolayevsk stay Russian until 1921. |
| Rail prevention versus later through traffic | Russia never completes its own through connection. Japan completes and controls the eastern link and terminal through Chinese-owned Manchuria; gauge and capacity are still proposed/audited. |
| “Common fleet” in 1919 before 1927 union | The peace regulates the combined allied fleets. Formal common ministries begin in 1927. |
| Kaiulani is called 93 and 94 in 1932 | Use 93–94 without inventing a birthday. Survival in Hawaii through the ending is settled. |
| The new heir and earlier Kalanimoku names | Keep the lost son and Julian/Chiyo’s heir distinct; retire former claimant, hypothetical parent and 1914 emperor roles without remapping old profiles. |
| Fleet-General Kuroda and Kiyoshi | Same active person. Keep CT-KURODA-II as resolved alias provenance; give Kiyoshi’s son a separate ID. |

New historical anchors in the attachments are recorded as source-supplied comparisons. This merge does not independently verify railway capacities, historical casualty totals, constitutional precedents, the intended London raid or financial analogues.
