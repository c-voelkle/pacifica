## Precedence decisions

The attachment contains approved later choices alongside older proposed replacements and intermediate calculations. The merge follows its final status and section E rather than copying section C verbatim.

- B1–B15 are approved except superseded B4. All 23 OPEN-020 entries are decided or superseded; OPEN-020-16 is superseded. No new CR-020 approval is pending.
- The final B15/D case supplies the reported scale: 25.6 million residents plus 1.1 million guest workers in 1929; Pacifica $51.6 billion, Japan $20.6 billion, Union $71.2 billion and the order $83.9 billion. Intermediate populations, ratios and income figures do not coexist as current estimates. China is outside the order's combined total.
- Crown equity is 25% in chartered/concession industries, 8% plus a golden share in competitive industries, and none in banking. Section C's abbreviated ownership wording is replaced by this final decision.
- The oil compact occurs in 1932, after the 1931 failure, following B5's explicit delay. The older 1931 policy-table and model-switch wording is retained only in the source record.
- B14's provision of Japan's full 1927 crisis borrowing supersedes B3's earlier 80% share. Its richer-economy scale also supersedes the smaller intermediate debt case.
- The approved 0.3% skilled-entry ceiling and later supply limitation supersede B10's binding 0.2% ceiling.
- The supplied Kāneʻohe Bay, Living Bay and Waimanu details are integrated. CR-021 itself was not provided; no additional decisions are inferred from its cross-references.
- CR-019 A7/J1 and OPEN-019-09, and the labor outcome in OPEN-018-19, are superseded. Labor registration leaves the main narrative; guest permits stay administrative background.
- CR-020 resolves Hoover's Pacifican identity, 1927 flood relief and June 1931 moratorium. Exact relief-to-treasury appointments under OPEN-019-04 remain qualified. Unrelated OPEN-018-18/20 and other earlier open mechanics remain open.
