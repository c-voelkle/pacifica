## Chichibu's character arc

The revised source adds F2. Chichibu studies at the Polytechnic of Carquinez in 1925–1926, then builds his outdoorsman reputation in the Sierra and Hawaiian uplands. Pacifican companions and local guides give him relationships beyond court; Crown honors, press and newsreels make his field life publicly familiar. His recognition is earned in the realm, rather than imported from a British education or European career.

His enjoyment and desire for field competence remain personal even as the courts make his reputation an argument for the Concord. As resident prince he represents Hirohito between Hayama Councils, with a place beside the throne at the Senate's opening Season. He is not assigned a common ministry or independent military command. His prominence gives the Restoration circles a visible Japanese royal target.

Specific decorations, named climbs and companions remain to be developed; no new order, military rank or summit record is invented. The 1925–1926 Carquinez study and Sierra/Hawaiian field life provide the narrative implementation of the author's direction.
