## Decisions and source reconciliation

All 17 OPEN-021 entries are approved. The header's full approval and final revision-8/K wording govern older proposed labels, the earlier capital sites and presidential styles. Master decision items 11, 12, 19, 21 and the Formosan part of 22 are marked resolved. Unrelated earlier questions stay qualified.

The following source tensions are handled explicitly:

- **Young's term:** I7 calls him President for the rescue. The existing approved sequence still places Dawes in office during the 1932 rescue/conference and Young's election in November. Young accepts the order as president-elect; his inauguration remains in 1933. No earlier presidency is invented.
- **Northern coast:** I5 supersedes the earlier Japanese recipient of Kamchatka and the Okhotsk coast. Those regions now belong to Siberia; detailed maps and the older separate Nikolayevsk clause remain identified for reconciliation.
- **Signing and travel:** the 1932 renewal acts are signed/promulgated at Thalassia; that does not place Hirohito there. Councils stay at Hayama, and his only Pacifica visit/dive is as crown prince in 1921.
- **Prince and dynasty:** the resident prince's office is approved but Chichibu is a candidate; Chiyo remains kazoku/Kuroda without newly invented imperial ancestry. The collateral-imperial betrothal connects the heir to Japan without merging crowns.
- **Tongue-vote cap:** full approval of the proposed sections covers C3/K4's uncapped option. Certificates add votes beyond English, alongside the one to three ordinary votes.

The working CR-020 copy has its B7 reef sentence corrected, as CR-021 J requests. The archived original CR-020 and its embedded original source text remain unchanged.
