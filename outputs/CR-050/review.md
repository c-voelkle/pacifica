# CR-050 — fresh-context reviews (3 November 2026)

## 1. Historical fact-check

An independent agent, given only `CR-050.md`, checked every claim presented as historical against published sources. It found no outright errors. Twelve claims needed a qualifier, and all twelve were corrected in CR-050 before integration:

| # | Claim | Correction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Soddy's 1904 "conclusion" that the energy can never be tapped | His view was that nature alone sets the rate and his hope that nature would guard her secret; his 1909 book was optimistic |
| 2 | Härting and Hesse identified lung cancer (1879) | They described a malignant lung tumour, classed as lymphosarcoma, later confirmed as lung cancer |
| 3 | Light quanta resisted until Millikan and Compton | Millikan confirmed Einstein's equation (1914–1916) but still rejected light quanta; acceptance followed Compton (1923) |
| 4 | Langmuir built the first true high-vacuum valves | Simultaneous with Harold Arnold at Western Electric, whom the courts held to have anticipated him |
| 5 | Dempster in Germany from 1910, came home in 1914 | In Germany from about 1911; went to the United States in 1914 |
| 6 | Hillebrand dies in Honolulu "as historically" | The date is historical; the place was Washington. Honolulu is the divergence |
| 7 | Hillebrand's helium episode | In-universe only; no change |
| 8 | Vienna's Radium Institute 1910 under Stefan Meyer | Funded 1908, opened 1910, Meyer acting director under Exner |
| 9 | Breeders dropped in the 1950s | Pursued into the 1980s; dropped as uranium proved plentiful and breeders costly |
| 10 | The element-94 plant "as large again" as the separation plant | Of comparable scale (Hanford about $390M, Y-12 about $478M) |
| 11 | Europe confirmed fission "within months" in 1939 | Within days to weeks |
| 12 | The papers "Fermi and Szilard mailed" | The two Columbia papers (Fermi's group; Szilard and Zinn), 16 March 1939 |

Also qualified: Szilard as "the chief advocate of voluntary secrecy in 1939" rather than its inventor; the great-circle midpoint lies south of the Aleutians. Sources are in the CR-050 section of `book-agent/revision/historical_background.md`.
