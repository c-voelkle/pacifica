## 2. Harbor law

The Basalt Harbor Charter has been in force unchanged since 1889 and remains unchanged through 1906. Only a recorded majority vote of its board can dismiss the Basalt Harbor master. The sovereign has no independent, delegated or emergency removal authority under this charter or any other applicable rule. The text describes legal authority, not the sovereign's physical ability to issue an unlawful command.

Under the original Whitecap rule of 1899 only its licensing board could issue fishing licenses. An approved amendment effective 1 January 1907 gives the sovereign independent authority to issue emergency Whitecap licenses. That amendment has no effect on Basalt Harbor.

## 3. Sailing record

S-1 is the Swallow named in the synopsis. With the route, propulsion and conditions used in June 1904, it takes at least twelve elapsed days to travel directly from Eastport to Westport. There is no faster route, extraordinary propulsion, calendar discrepancy or recorded exceptional passage. The same passengers stay aboard throughout. This minimum applies to that actual voyage.

## 4. Cipher receipt

Cora receives and understands the complete harbor cipher at noon on 2 May 1906. She remains alive, remembers the entire cipher, and knows it continuously through 4 May. She is a distinct person from Arin and Bela. Daro merely knows that the cipher exists; he does not know its contents.

## 5. Financial record

The final audited Harbor Office unrestricted-fund account for the fiscal year ending 31 December 1904 uses millions of the same crown currency throughout. Opening cash is 10, cash receipts are 4, cash expenditure is 6 and closing cash is 8. These are all movements of the fund; there are no transfers, adjustments or omitted entries. No restricted reserve is included. The numbers reconcile as 10 + 4 - 6 = 8.

The separate exhibition account reports gross receipts 12, costs 4 and net receipts 8. Its restricted reserve is 4. That account does not assert an equality with the Harbor Office fund balance.
