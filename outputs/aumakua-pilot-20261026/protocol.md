# Aumakua first behavioral evaluation

26 October 2026

The author requested a separate skill for consistency across the text after the ontology-enabled Aegis pass omitted two textual questions found by an earlier review. Aumakua's objective is internal and cross-document consistency, separate from Aegis's effective-power comparison. Its workflow reads raw prose before extracted graphs and reconciles prior findings after a fresh sweep.

The first skill version was written before either test below. Two independent evaluating agents received the skill, a realistic review request and raw source artifacts. Neither received an intended answer, prior assessment, ontology dataset, issue register or another evaluator's output. The parent retained the known questions and synthetic answer key separately until the reviews were complete. Evaluation outputs were generated outside the repository, then copied here for review. Source canon was not edited.

## Pacifica prose pilot

The evaluator was asked to audit current master storyline sections 4–8 for internal and cross-document consistency, using repository authority and relevant current approval/identity records. The base narrative is the same one used by the earlier Aegis comparison. The evaluator could inspect raw canonical counterparts beyond those sections, but not earlier review outputs. This is a bounded review, not a claim to have read or certified the entire novel. The manifest records actual input files and hashes; the review distinguishes direct reading from targeted searches.

After completion, the parent checks whether the review independently raises the two withheld questions: the gold-secrecy membership count and the Council of State's chronology. A qualified finding counts as a detection: these questions require scope and authority analysis, rather than an advance assumption that each is a proven contradiction. Additional findings are reviewed on their own evidence.

## Fresh fictional fixture

The second evaluator was given only `fixture/story.md`, `fixture/records.md` and the skill. The test text is entirely fictional and is not proposed Pacifica canon. Both fixture files have equal current authority, and provide enough information to evaluate each comparison without external research.

The parent prepared six incompatible pairs involving voyage duration, a cash balance, exclusive dismissal authority, kinship, age and knowledge membership. Eight compatible controls cover financial bases, an explicit amendment, distinct identities, an intentional lie, distinct predecessor/successor institutions, a pending proposal and an unresolved mystery. An additional knowledge-scope control distinguishes awareness that a cipher exists from knowledge of its contents. The key was withheld from the evaluator.

The parent checks detected seeded pairs, unsupported conflict flags on the compatible controls, calibration, and evidence/repair quality. Counts are descriptive results for this fixture, not a general accuracy estimate. No model-only baseline was run on this fixture, and this evaluation cannot isolate a causal effect of the skill or predict whole-book recall.
