# Aumakua: first test results

26 October 2026

Aumakua independently recovered both textual questions omitted by the ontology-enabled Aegis rerun. On a separate fictional fixture it detected all six planted conflicts and did not flag the eight compatible controls as conflicts. These results support using the skill for a dedicated consistency pass; they do not establish whole-book recall or a causal improvement over unassisted review.

The [protocol](protocol.md) explains the withheld findings, fixed sources and evaluation limits. The skill was not revised after seeing either result. Its instructions, ledger reference and UI metadata passed the official skill validator; installation hashes were checked for all three Aumakua files and Aegis's small routing update. No narrative, approval, identity or financial source was changed.

Read the [independent review](actual/review.md) for paired passages, classifications, repair options and coverage. The continuous sweep covers master storyline §§4–8, with selected setup, later counterparts, approvals and character records. It is not an audit of the whole novel.

Three further entries distinguish different kinds of problem. AUM-4-8-003 flags Abigail's unverifiable deathbed account becoming unqualified narration in §6 as an integration/stance mismatch, without deciding what really happened. These additional entries have useful paired evidence and calibrated conclusions; they are not three more confirmed factual contradictions or adopted revisions.

The review also preserves compatible readings: the superseded 1857 marriage date, Hawaiian domestic signature versus common assent, distinct armored/turret ship classes, and an explicitly open Perry premise. Agreement between the bible, registry and prose is recognized as potentially copied evidence, rather than independent corroboration.

All eight compatible controls were treated as compatible: gross/net receipts, restricted/unrestricted cash, amended Whitecap authority, two Iona Reeds, Rook's intentional lie, distinct predecessor/successor councils, the proposed lighthouse transfer and the unknown key thief. Daro's awareness that a cipher exists is also correctly distinguished from knowledge of its contents. The six material findings map to the six seeded conflicts; no additional unsupported conflict was asserted on this fixture.

This is a small, explicitly specified test. Several exclusions were written into the fixture to make each expected comparison determinate. No skill-free baseline or repeated-trial estimate was run, and both skill and fixture were authored in response to a known failure class. The outcome does not demonstrate general precision, exhaustive recall, or that separating skills alone causes better performance.

## What is ready to use

Invoke `$aumakua` for a suspected contradiction, continuity check or cross-document consistency audit. It reads prose first, records paired evidence and actual coverage, and consults prior issues after the independent sweep. The compact ontology is optional; its structural validation cannot certify prose coverage. Aegis routes consistency requests to Aumakua while retaining its own effective-power objective.

The archived input manifests retain the exact paths and raw-byte hashes from each run. Fixture and output copies here preserve those bytes; the parent integrity record maps them to this archive. Source hashes pin versions, not claims of whole-file reading. Installation verification is a dated execution record, separate from the earlier Aegis comparison's historical skill-version record.
