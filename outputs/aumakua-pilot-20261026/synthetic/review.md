## Issue ledger

### AUM-SYN-001 — Sole lawful dismissal conflicts with exclusive board authority

**Classification:** Integration mismatch / confirmed contradiction. **Lifecycle:** Open. **Consequence:** High; changes whether the central dismissal is lawful. **Evidence strength:** Explicit on both sides.

- **S1, C, line 17:** “lawfully dismisses” and “through her sole personal order”; “The board neither votes nor delegates a power”.
- **S2, 2, line 15:** “Only a recorded majority vote of its board can dismiss the Basalt Harbor master” and “The sovereign has no independent, delegated or emergency removal authority”.

**Aligned scope and conflict:** The same Basalt office holder is dismissed in August 1905 under the charter unchanged from 1889 through 1906. The prose calls this lawful while excluding the required board action and every stated alternative source of authority. A sovereign's ability to give an unlawful order does not supply lawful removal authority.

**Compatible readings and uncertainty:** An unlawful dismissal would be physically possible, but S1 expressly says lawful. A delegated power, emergency, amendment or separate instrument is expressly excluded. Whitecap's 1907 amendment governs a different institution. No compatible reading is supported under the existing commitments; what remains undecided is which account the author wishes to preserve.

**Minimal repair proposals:** If the charter rule is retained, change S1 line 17 to make the dismissal unlawful, or replace its sole-order/no-board description with the required recorded majority decision. If a lawful sole order is essential, revise the governing Basalt rule and corresponding exclusions in both S1 line 17 and S2 line 15. These are alternative author choices, not assumed off-page events.

### AUM-SYN-002 — Full biological sister conflicts with complete only-child record

**Classification:** Contradiction. **Lifecycle:** Open. **Consequence:** High; affects identity and family relationships. **Evidence strength:** Explicit on both sides.

- **S1, D, line 21:** Merra welcomes her “younger full biological sister Nila Vale, N-05”; “They share both biological parents, Dain Vale and Irena Moss”.
- **S2, 1, line 8:** Merra is “the only child ever born to either Dain Vale or Irena Moss”; “Only child” covers “their complete family”.
- **Identity safeguard, S2 line 9:** Nila has “No alternate identity or impersonation”.

**Aligned scope and conflict:** N-04 and N-05 are distinct, living people in April 1906. Two full biological daughters imply at least two children born to each named parent, incompatible with exactly one ever born to either parent. “Younger” cannot change this lifetime membership claim.

**Compatible readings and uncertainty:** Adoption, social sisterhood, a household-limited count, an earlier date and a cover identity are explicitly excluded. No supported reconciliation remains; the intended genealogy is unresolved between these documents.

**Minimal repair proposals:** If full biological sisterhood is retained, remove or revise S2 line 8's only-child claim. If the only-child record is retained, revise the relationship and shared-parent assertions in S1 line 21 together, leaving the replacement relationship for the author to choose. Recheck any later genealogy when that choice is made.

### AUM-SYN-003 — Exclusive cipher knowledge omits a living third knower

**Classification:** Contradiction. **Lifecycle:** Open. **Consequence:** High; affects secrecy and who could act on the cipher. **Evidence strength:** Explicit temporal overlap and distinct identities.

- **S1, F, line 29:** “At noon on 3 May 1906, only Arin and Bela know the full harbor cipher”; the scope is “everyone alive, wherever located”.
- **S2, 4, line 25:** “Cora receives and understands the complete harbor cipher at noon on 2 May 1906” and “knows it continuously through 4 May”; she is “a distinct person from Arin and Bela”.

**Aligned scope and conflict:** Noon on 3 May falls inside Cora's continuous knowledge interval. The record establishes at least three full-cipher knowers at that instant, while S1 restricts the set to exactly Arin and Bela. This is full knowledge, not awareness that a cipher exists.

**Compatible readings and uncertainty:** Room membership, publicly acknowledged knowledge, an alias, Cora's death or forgetting cannot reconcile the passages because the scope and record exclude them. Daro's mere awareness is compatible and does not add another full-cipher knower. No compatible reading is supported; the author must decide the intended knowledge distribution.

**Minimal repair proposals:** If Cora's receipt is retained, add Cora to S1 line 29's exclusive list, or remove the exclusivity claim while preserving Arin and Bela's knowledge. If exclusivity is essential, revise Cora's receipt/continuous-knowledge timing in S2 line 25 so it does not include the stated noon. Recheck any dependent secrecy scenes after the choice.

### AUM-SYN-004 — Direct voyage dates violate the voyage-specific minimum

**Classification:** Contradiction, chronology/duration. **Lifecycle:** Open. **Consequence:** Material; arrival-based chronology cannot hold as written. **Evidence strength:** Explicit same-voyage alignment.

- **S1, A, line 7:** S-1 “leaves Eastport on 1 June 1904” and “arrives there on 5 June 1904”, directly, with passengers aboard throughout.
- **S2, 3, line 21:** Under that voyage's actual route, propulsion and conditions, it “takes at least twelve elapsed days”; “This minimum applies to that actual voyage”.

**Aligned scope and conflict:** These are the same S-1, passengers, actual voyage, calendar and endpoint events. The calendar dates differ by four days. Exact times of day are absent, so an exact 96-hour duration is not established; even the widest possible interval between those dates is under five elapsed days, below twelve.

**Compatible readings and uncertainty:** Message dates, a faster route, exceptional propulsion, intermediate travel and calendar differences are excluded. Time-of-day uncertainty cannot close the gap. The intended departure, arrival or travel rule is the remaining author choice.

**Minimal repair proposals:** If departure and minimum are retained, arrival must be 13 June or later, with times allowing twelve elapsed days. If 5 June arrival is retained, move departure early enough to allow that minimum. Alternatively, revise the voyage-specific duration premise in S2 line 21 and its exclusions. Recheck S1 section A and any arrival-dependent chronology after choosing.

### AUM-SYN-005 — Two closing balances for one final unrestricted account

**Classification:** Numerical discrepancy / confirmed integration mismatch. **Lifecycle:** Open. **Consequence:** Material; changes available cash by four million crowns. **Evidence strength:** Explicit common account, date, units and final version.

- **S1, B, line 11:** The final unrestricted fund account has closing cash “twelve million crowns”; it is “the same fund and final account kept in the financial record”.
- **S2, 5, line 29:** “Opening cash is 10, cash receipts are 4, cash expenditure is 6 and closing cash is 8”; “10 + 4 - 6 = 8”.

**Aligned scope and conflict:** Both refer to the Harbor Office unrestricted fund, fiscal year ending 31 December 1904, millions of the same crowns, final audited version, excluding restricted reserves. The stated complete movements yield eight million, not twelve. The difference is four million crowns.

**Compatible readings and uncertainty:** Gross versus net, a different fund, a later revision or adding the restricted reserve would ordinarily warrant checking, but S1 line 11 and S2 line 29 expressly prevent those reconciliations. The separate exhibition account does not explain the fund conflict. Which amount is intended remains an author decision.

**Minimal repair proposals:** If the internally reconciled record is retained, change S1 line 11's closing balance from twelve to eight million. If twelve is intended, revise S2 line 29's balance and at least one underlying movement consistently, updating the equation; no omitted transfer may be silently assumed. Keep the exhibition figures separate.

### AUM-SYN-006 — Birthday age conflicts with definite birth date

**Classification:** Numerical discrepancy / confirmed contradiction. **Lifecycle:** Open. **Consequence:** Local continuity error, potentially wider if ages drive later events. **Evidence strength:** Exact identity and matching day/month.

- **S1, E, line 25:** “On 12 May 1906, Tavi Noll, person N-01, celebrates his twenty-fourth birthday”.
- **S2, 1, line 7:** “N-01: Tavi Noll, born 12 May 1887”; the birth date is definite and uses the same calendar.

**Aligned scope and conflict:** The same person reaches his birthday on the stated date: 1906 − 1887 = 19. A twenty-fourth birthday on 12 May 1906 implies a birth year of 1882, five years earlier than the record.

**Compatible readings and uncertainty:** The narrator gives the age as fact; adopted birthdays and calendar conventions are explicitly excluded. No supported age-counting reconciliation remains. The author has not selected which datum to change.

**Minimal repair proposals:** Retain the birth date and change “twenty-fourth” to “nineteenth” in S1 line 25; or retain the twenty-fourth birthday and change S2 line 7's birth year to 1882, checking dependent ages. Neither change is made by this audit.
