## 2. New regressions in v2

**(a) Julian/Noe trajectory sentence at the paragraph end (line 850). Regression; the repair is needed for the protected mystery.** It now reads "…and that Noe's death had more suspects than he knew. In 1888 Noe refuses to be the loyalists' banner, turns some of their chiefs toward her son and wins the Queen's undertaking…, then returns to the typhoid wards." The jump back from 1915 to 1888 reads as a gloss on the previous clause. Its three verbs name exactly the parties on line 511's motive list (the loyalists she refused, the chiefs she drew away, the Queen she pressed), and the sentence ends on typhoid. A reader takes it as the explanation of "more suspects". This is H4's placement problem again, with motives instead of documents. The character bible requires the death not to make "Victoria guilty by implication."
*Smallest repair:* move the sentence after the paragraph's first sentence ("…give her agency independent of the men and dynasty."), where it illustrates her agency and stays away from the death. This needs the return-marker convention for unlabelled text (AUM-12, an **author item**). If the author does not accept that convention, cut the addition. §23 and §29 already carry the move. Leaving it at the end is not acceptable.

**(b) Victoria trajectory sentence after the Donner/Altai close (line 838). Regression; literary preference, not a mystery issue.** "…as someone who has kept one" is the paragraph's moral summary; v2 follows it with a step back to 1888 and ends on procedure. The sentence also misfires: "choose between a Hawaiian court she once failed and failing it again" sets a noun against an action. It also repeats "choose" and "by choice".
*Smallest repair:* place it after "Her rivalry with Noe gives the measures an emotional foundation." That is the natural place, but it also needs the AUM-12 convention (**author item**). If it stays at the end, at least fix the grammar: "choose between honouring a Hawaiian court she once failed and failing it again in public". Cutting it costs little.
*Note:* one author decision on return markers for unlabelled canon (AUM-12) fixes both (a) and (b). Without it, cutting both trajectory additions is the cleanest result.

**(c) "The two halves" separated from "his adoption by a Kamehameha queen" (line 638). Minor; mostly a gain, with one new ambiguity.** Because it names "the descent and the adoption" again, the distance does not matter. It now reads as an answer to the loyalists' hānai objection, which is a gain, though it softens the unreversed-1876 sting just before it. The new defect is a garden-path phrase: "his claim that Noe refused to separate" can be read as "his claim [i.e. assertion] that Noe refused".
*Repair (literary):* "Descent and adoption are the two halves of his claim; in 1888 Noe refused to separate them."

**(d) §29 sentences before "He tells no one that he knows." (line 662). Close lands; one small repair is needed for the mystery.** The section close is restored. But "…they do not show how she died. He tells no one that he knows." places the open "knows" right after the death. A quick reader can hear "knows [how she died]", which hints that Julian learned something about the death.
*Smallest repair (word order only):* "They do not show how she died; they show what Noe bargained for him." Then "he knows" follows the bargain and refers back to Donner and the bargain.

**(e) §23 length. Unchanged trade-off, not a new regression; partly resolved.** The inserts total about 510 words (173 + 337), against about 320 for the four baseline §23 paragraphs and 175 for Donner; the second insert alone outweighs the rest of the section. The paragraph count fell but the words did not, because the repairs added clauses (M2, M4, M5, M8). The bargain still outweighs the death and registers the section is named for. *Trims (literary preference, author choice), which keep every H4 fix:*
- End the paragraph "…vaccination in Honolulu and Maui and the district registers." Dropping "in the autumn, typhoid" removes the echo of the next sentence ("in the autumn of 1888 during a typhoid outbreak") and stops the bargain paragraph running straight into the death.
- Cut "Victoria has leverage of her own: … the purse of Noe's board." down to "Victoria has leverage of her own." if the leverage stays unused.
- Or split the second paragraph at "Victoria has leverage of her own".

**(f) New ambiguity in Victoria's gain (§23 paragraph 2). Small regression; clarity.** "For a narrow price she gets Noe's public refusal of the loyalists and the chiefs Noe has won, and an heir of the Council's making would be no more hers than Noe's." This can parse as Noe refusing both the loyalists and the chiefs. The second clause also breaks the structure, as though the heir were part of what Victoria gets.
*Repair:* "For a narrow price she gets the chiefs Noe has won and Noe's public refusal of the loyalists; besides, an heir of the Council's making would be no more hers than Noe's."
