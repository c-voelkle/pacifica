# The Harbor Realm: current synopsis

## 1. The heir

Jani, stable identity T-JANI, is born on 12 March 1887. Her mother Nell dies in 1900 in circumstances the narrative deliberately leaves unresolved. Jani suspects political interference but has no proof and does not know who, if anyone, caused the death. Captain Sora, T-SORA, remains her demanding ally rather than her subordinate.

## 2. The two ports unite

On 31 July 1906, nineteen-year-old Jani signs the Treaty of Two Ports with Governor Eren. After four months of bargaining, both assemblies ratify in November 1906. Only then does the United Council come into existence. It controls common naval procurement, but each port retains taxation and its own armed forces. The Council's first mandate lasts five years, from November 1906 until November 1911. A new mandate requires both assemblies' agreement.

## 3. Credit and refusal

In 1907 the United Council issues its first loan to improve repair yards and fuel stores. Eren approves only after obtaining a ceiling on his port's liability and a veto over spending outside common defense. Sora says that a ship without trained crews and a supply chain is an ornament. Her support depends on protecting those expenditures. The synopsis supplies no numerical construction budget or borrowing capacity.

## 4. The distant harbor

In May 1909 advisers propose leasing a permanent naval establishment at East Haven, far beyond the two ports. Its host offers access in peacetime but reserves wartime decisions and seeks a defense commitment. Annual operating cost, reinforcement capacity and comparative returns are unknown. The lease is still a proposal. Existing commercial repair contracts and agreements for occasional visits could be expanded instead, but their wartime reliability also requires investigation.

## 5. A commemoration

At the ceremony of 31 July 1910, Jani recalls the four years since she signed the treaty. She wants to secure a second Council mandate before November 1911, but Eren has not yet agreed. Nell's letters make her reconsider a youthful certainty about the death; they do not establish a culprit. Sora's continued cooperation requires a bargain, not merely a title.
