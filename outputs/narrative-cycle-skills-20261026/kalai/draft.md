# CR draft: Treaty of Two Ports signed in 1908

Status: PROPOSED standalone alternative. This draft covers the complete supplied synopsis, through the ceremony of 31 July 1910. It has not been integrated into canon.

## Revised synopsis

Jani, T-JANI, is born on 12 March 1887. Her mother Nell dies in 1900, leaving a loss whose circumstances remain unresolved. Jani suspects political interference, but suspicion gives her neither proof nor knowledge of who, if anyone, caused Nell's death. Captain Sora, T-SORA, is her demanding ally. Sora's cooperation is something Jani must earn and sustain; it does not make Sora her subordinate.

On 31 July 1908, twenty-one-year-old Jani signs the Treaty of Two Ports with Governor Eren. Four months of bargaining follow before both assemblies ratify it in November 1908. Only with that ratification does the United Council come into existence. The ports entrust it with common naval procurement while retaining their own taxation and armed forces. Its first mandate runs for five years, from November 1908 until November 1913. A second Council mandate will require the agreement of both assemblies.

The Council's first loan finances improvements to repair yards and fuel stores. Eren makes his approval depend on a ceiling on his port's liability and a veto over expenditure outside common defense. Sora insists that ships need trained crews and a supply chain; her support depends on protecting those expenditures alongside the material improvements. The borrowing therefore expresses a bargain among actors who retain the means to withhold cooperation. **[OPEN: the loan must occur after the Council's creation in November 1908. Its new date, and whether it precedes the May 1909 proposal below, have not been selected.]**

In May 1909, advisers propose leasing a permanent naval establishment at East Haven, far beyond the two ports. The host offers peacetime access, reserves its wartime decisions, and seeks a defense commitment. A permanent establishment would thus bring obligations without assuring Jani that she could use it when war made access most valuable. The advisers' proposal remains unapproved. Its annual operating cost, the capacity to reinforce it, and its returns compared with other uses of resources are unknown. Existing commercial repair contracts and agreements for occasional visits offer another course through expansion, though their reliability in wartime also remains to be investigated. No choice between these courses has been settled.

At the ceremony of 31 July 1910, Jani recalls the two years since she signed the treaty. She wants to secure a second Council mandate before the first expires in November 1913, but Eren has not yet agreed. The authority created by the treaty still depends on bargains with people and assemblies able to refuse her. Sora's continued cooperation likewise requires an agreement that protects what she considers necessary, rather than merely a title. Meanwhile Nell's letters make Jani reconsider a youthful certainty about her mother's death. They change her interpretation without establishing a culprit, and the mystery remains unresolved.
