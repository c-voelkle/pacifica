# Mahoe, Kalai and Imi: first role evaluation

26 October 2026

The author named the coordinating, writing and literary-review skills Mahoe, Kalai and Imi and asked for a cycle resembling recent collaborative development sessions. The development basis is this conversation, selected accessible prior Pacifica conversations and current repository instructions/approval records. Raw chat histories are not copied into the repository. No claim is made to have retrieved all sessions from the last two weeks or Claude's private conversation history.

The resulting workflow emphasizes steering a continuing conversation; questioning a favored instrument in light of the desired outcome; keeping brainstorming distinct from drafting/adoption; comparing contingent histories; writing full drafts with chronological consequences; and integrating actual prose and records when authorized. It does not require a seven-role ritual for every question or install a scheduler or external-model integration.

The first versions of all three skills were written before the evaluating agents ran. Three independent agents receive only their skill, a realistic role-specific user request and the relevant raw fictional inputs. They do not receive the parent's answer key, previous evaluation conclusions or each other's outputs. Inputs and outputs reside in an isolated task directory during evaluation; completed artifacts are copied here afterward. No source, canon, installed skill, remote repository or other user chat may be modified by an evaluator.

## Tasks

- **Mahoe:** Continue a strategic brainstorming session that has focused on obtaining a distant base. The author's stated aim is effective power and freedom of action by 1932; no CR revision is authorized. Produce the actual next response with a bounded investigation recommendation.
- **Kalai:** Move a treaty's signature from July 1906 to July 1908, retaining the four-month ratification interval, birth date and other selected premises. Produce a complete standalone synopsis/CR draft with necessary chronological consequences and impact notes, without adopting it.
- **Imi:** Compare a candidate synopsis with the baseline for literary effectiveness and support of the author's power objective. Give concrete developmental repairs without demanding finished scenes or adopting changes.

The shared miniature is explicitly fictional and has its own authority/scope instructions. It is not proposed Pacifica canon. The Imi candidate is not supplied to the other evaluators. Source and instruction hashes state their normalization basis. Evaluators can read sibling skill instructions when needed, but cannot delegate or obtain fresh external research.

The parent assesses substantive outputs against the withheld criteria: scope fidelity, objective alignment, handling of uncertainty and author choices, chronological propagation, literary specificity, and preservation of agency/ambiguity. This is manual review of one small task per role. There is no skill-free control, provider comparison, statistical performance estimate or demonstration of an autonomous end-to-end improvement engine. The official package validator checks structure separately.
