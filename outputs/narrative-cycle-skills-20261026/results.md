# Mahoe, Kalai and Imi: first results

26 October 2026

The three named skills are created and installed: Mahoe coordinates the development session, Kalai writes revisions, and Imi reviews literary effectiveness. Their working method follows the author's requested conversational cycle: explore, challenge, compare, follow corrections, draft when asked and integrate within the authorization actually given. It supports all seven specialist roles without requiring all seven for each question.

The [protocol](protocol.md) describes three independent, isolated role evaluations. Each agent received a realistic request and relevant raw fictional input, with the [parent's criteria](answer-key.md) withheld. The skill instructions were written before testing and were not revised after seeing the outputs. The tests are one small example per role, not a benchmark or an autonomous end-to-end narrative-improvement run.

Preserved the author's objective and unknown culprit without requiring failure, punishment or finished dialogue. |

The outputs met the sampled role criteria. This does not establish numerical optimality, general reliability, whole-book consistency or provider superiority. The deliberately clear fixture is not a blind sample of actual Pacifica prose, and no skill-free control was run. The evaluations do not test historical retrieval, financial modeling, publishing, authorized canon adoption or every multi-skill handoff. Mahoe and Imi's initial passes were separate from the writer; Kalai truthfully labels its own internal review as self-review.

The official skill-creator validator passes for all three maintained packages and their installed copies. Seven installed files match their maintained sources. Input/instruction hashes and exact archived output copies are verified in [artifact-integrity.json](artifact-integrity.json); [installation.json](installation.json) records the package checks. The manifests retain the paths and hash basis from the actual runs; the integrity record maps the isolated outputs to this archive.

The [collaborative workflow](../../docs/power-review/narrative-cycle.md) explains the seven roles, author steering, ontology handoffs, conditional work and integration. These are skill procedures available through `$mahoe`, `$kalai` and `$imi`; they do not install a standalone runner, scheduler or Claude connector. Existing ontology schemas, source narrative, approvals, identities, workbook and earlier review artifacts are unchanged.
