# Pacifica power success contract

Version 0.1.0 · 26 October 2026 · Author-directed evaluation objective

## Governing objective

An adjustment succeeds when, compared with a specified feasible alternative, it credibly strengthens the trajectory toward Julian's eventual effective capacity to secure obedience, mobilize resources, direct institutions and shape other actors' choices. Explain how the gain reaches him, the conditions under which he can exercise it, accompanying burdens and the security of its persistence.

The principal endpoint is 1932. Use intermediate checkpoints appropriate to the causal chain, including 1887 and 1914–1915 when succession matters. Endpoint success is an assessed consequence, never an input imposed to validate an earlier decision.

This contract expresses the author's worldbuilding objective. Earlier actors retain their own goals and contemporary information. It does not approve plot changes or treat increasing command as a moral endorsement. Human and ecological consequences remain visible; they affect this power assessment where a mechanism connects them to capacity, support, resistance or obligations, or where the author sets a separate constraint.

## What is being compared

Pin baseline and alternative, authority/version, earliest divergence, horizon, affected institutions, coverage and shared external assumptions before evaluating. A feasible alternative has specified legal/political and physical/financial prerequisites; unresolved feasibility yields a conditional comparison. Retain a baseline option. Clarifying an existing mechanism may improve explanatory support without increasing simulated power.

Separate an authored-history consequence, a calculated conditional consequence and robustness across plausible worlds. Never give a historical probability or universal optimum from a selected example. A new political or historical premise is a new case; do not retain incompatible downstream outcomes.

## Power outcomes

Assess four outcomes without automatic aggregation:
1. Resources Julian can actually mobilize after existing claims and obligations.
2. Decisions he can implement at specified scale, speed, geographical reach and reliability.
3. His leverage over independent actors who retain alternatives and refusal.
4. His freedom to act despite external obstruction, domestic vetoes and indispensable intermediaries.

Specify person, office, institution, jurisdiction, date, instrument, conditions and counterparty for each material claim. Distinguish authority from practical control, ownership from spendable cash, a promise from delivery, common coordination from annexation and government credit from private claims. Wealth, GDP, bullion output and allied resources are inputs or context, not measures of Julian's command by themselves.

## Causal chain and burdens

Trace five links:
- Creation: what additional resource, capability, right or dependency is established?
- Preservation: how does it survive maintenance, opposition, crisis and institutional change?
- Transmission: how does Julian acquire usable control or dependable cooperation?
- Exercise: what can he actually cause to happen, and who can still refuse?
- Accumulation: what subsequent undertaking becomes feasible or easier?

Identify quantities where supported, time lags, operating and capital burdens, debt service, contingent liabilities, resource competition, partner/rival responses and reversibility. Do not count the same gain again as resource, mechanism and outcome. Earlier institutional restraint can preserve future capacity; immediate executive discretion need not rise at every step.

The legal lapse of Elias's enabling delegation in 1887 is not institutional collapse and is not automatic inheritance. Trace surviving establishments, their controllers, the regency bargain and Julian's accession separately. A maintained equilibrium may preserve power without conferring unilateral command.

## Evidence and judgments

Keep these assessments separate:
- Narrative link: explained, asserted, missing or contradicted, with passage locators.
- Historical support: supporting, conflicting, analogous, unavailable or not applicable, with precise scope.
- Quantitative support: independently calculated, inherited model result, conditional calculation or unmodeled.
- Sensitivity: the parameter, actor response or mechanism that could change the conclusion.

Authorial status, evidential basis and review result are distinct. Missing, false, absent and unmodeled are not interchangeable. A source describing an actual event does not establish a counterfactual response. Calculation correctness does not establish practical or historical feasibility.

Use verdicts improves, weakens, mixed or unresolved. Specify whether a verdict concerns Julian's power or only explanatory/evidential support. A material, unsupported transmission link prevents an unconditional endpoint improvement claim. State trade-offs and, where no weighting is supplied, retain incomparable alternatives. Use a null and a reason for unavailable quantities. Do not invent a 0–100 power score or uncalibrated probabilities.

## Review completion

A review is usable when the author can inspect the causal chain, comparison, principal costs, evidence, limitations and a concrete recommendation. A conditional or unresolved result is legitimate. Complete supported calculations and research; a list of missing inputs alone is insufficient when useful work remains possible.

Before finalizing, test for: wealth without command; capacity held by an independent institution; gross gain outweighed by obligations; expiry or failed succession; partner refusal; and a temporary restraint that protects later capacity. Record actual failures and revisions rather than claiming an independent evaluation occurred when the same analyst performed the pilot.

## Scope boundary

This is a prose evaluation contract, not an ontology, data schema or executable simulation. Existing Timeline Lab ontology files are unchanged. Future ontology design should follow demonstrated review needs.
