It is instructed to omit the profile README's case-specific pilot section. It builds and validates its own graph and writes an assessment. The parent reviewer subsequently compares the two outputs and checks the numerical scope. The evaluator does not publish, edit canon or alter the skills.

Compare actual outcomes: the recommendation and its basis; retention of local authorial qualifications; the distinction between ownership and usable cooperation; causal versus chronological support; the separation of evidence applicability and arithmetic; explicit conditions, burdens and refusal/expiry boundaries; preserved unknowns; and inspectable links from prose to records. Report missing coverage and integration friction as well as improvements. Counts describe the representation and are not quality scores.

This is one matched-source, separate-evaluator case study. The schema and workflow were designed with this case in view, and the old review was produced in a different pass. The comparison cannot isolate an ontology effect from changed instructions, evaluator variation or additional review attention. It does not establish general model performance, independence of historical sources or historical validity from SHACL conformance.
