Points marked "(my knowledge)" are the reviewer's own, not from the repository. The repair pass that followed is recorded in `review-set-fixes.md`.

## 1. Cross-CR contradictions
1. Fix: adopt CR-062's split; write O1 as a cession "as Oregon's voters choose under the protocol of [date]"; revise CR-059 V1, Part VI and CT14.
2. Deseret's status 1862–66 — CR-059 W10, route table (l.355) and offers row "Deseret a fellow member" (l.447) vs CR-060 D5/D12/CT1 (associate; member on exchange). Fix: apply CR-060 CT1's knock-ons to CR-059 now.

4. Senate packet vs charters created — CR-062 S1 omits western Montana (CR-060 A3), New Mexico (CR-061 A4), the San Luis Valley, Arizona (no consent instrument anywhere); T3/X2 omit Fort Garland (CR-061 P2). Fix: one charter table in CR-062 S1; Fort Garland in T3.
5. The Alabama flank — CR-064 A5: Polk's army at Blue Mountain, Johnston on the Chattahoochee; CR-063 Part VI/storyline: "Johnston on the Alabama flank". Fix: "Polk's army on the Alabama flank, Johnston on the Chattahoochee."
6. Vicksburg escapees — CR-064 "about 15,000 (CR-063 sets the number)"; CR-063 V3 sets none. Fix: CR-063 V3 states march-out and rear-garrison strengths (e.g. ~15,000 and ~8,000).

9. Morton — CR-065 E4 turns him out in October 1864; CR-062 storyline seats him in the 1867 opposition. Fix: name only Wade and Chandler.
10. Butler — CR-063 S4/S6 (12,000 recalled July 1862; 3,000 at Pensacola; Butler on the James from late 1863) agrees with CR-064 R3; CR-065 V2 hedges. Fix: CR-065 commits.
