# Fresh-context review 2: CR-060, CR-061 and CR-067 (13 November 2026)

Reviewer: a separate agent context given only AGENTS.md, the three drafts, the canon extract of §§8–13 and named rows of the neighbouring drafts. It did not see the session records or CR-068-record.md. Points marked "(my knowledge)" are the reviewer's own. Line numbers are those of commit 91739ce.

## Verdict

CR-060, CR-061 and CR-067 are not ready to integrate with the set. The main obstacle is Elias's whereabouts from November 1862 to May 1863: CR-061 and CR-068 give incompatible calendars, and CR-060's Compact, Bear River and 1863 renewal all depend on which is true. The second obstacle is CR-060's storyline text, which contradicts three author decisions.

## Blockers

1. **Elias's calendar, winter 1862–63.**
   - CR-061 C12 (l.60): he leaves Monterey "in November", is at "Taos in mid-December 1862", is "home in May (MODEL)", and the renewal "is voted by the member legislatures in his absence, on his written acceptance carried by courier".
   - CR-068 CT24 (l.804) and G4 (l.243): "About 5 January | Leaves Monterey"; "Elias has been home from Texas since March"; about 20 May "He speaks to the legislature".
   - CR-060 D9 (l.63) adopts CR-068: "passed narrowly in the last week of May after Elias answers at Honolulu".
   - CR-060 Part II also has "December 1862: both" (Young and Elias) settling the Compact while CR-061 has him at Taos.
   - Fix: keep CR-061's outward leg (Taos is integrated CR-066 canon). Bring him home by late April, delete "in his absence … by courier" and "seven months", and rewrite the first three rows of CT24's table. Name who signs the Compact for Pacifica.

2. **CT7 is deferred but still in the text; this is the protected-intent exposure.**
   - CT7 decision (l.368): "the Legion's pay and the Black Hawk War stay out of the storyline text."
   - §10 draft (l.264): the Legion guards the road "under its own command and on Pacifica's pay".
   - D7 states "on confederal pay in gold" unconditionally, and N6 lets the Legion fight the Black Hawk War as "Deseret's militia, not a confederal force". Together these are the draft's own Case A, which it says works round "no campaign of extermination".
   - Fix: strike the pay clause from l.264; mark D7, N6 and the "Legion's pay" model line as held pending CT7. Do not integrate them otherwise.

3. **CR-060's §10 storyline contradicts decided rows.**
   - l.269: "no annexation, Carson's guarantees kept, and Washoe to choose for itself after the peace". C6 (decided 12 November): she "does not get it written into the renewal" and wins "Hawaii's control of entry at its own ports". The text grants the lost condition and omits the won one.
   - l.264: "Connor's camp is named among the garrison's posts". D4: Connor is the officer the Defense Law "does not" send; the post is "the old Camp Floyd".
   - Fix: rewrite both sentences from C6 and D5.

4. **CR-068's "account of the nations" (February–March 1863) claims what CR-061 dates later.**
   - CR-068 l.239: "a treaty that recognizes the Diné homeland"; peonage and the captive trade "are being ended by its own law". CT15: the start date "is to be fixed in CR-061 so that it precedes September 1862".
   - CR-061: Cubero is signed "at the beginning of May 1863" for passage only; the homeland treaty is 1865; K4 ends peonage "on the exchange of the peace"; the ordinance is December 1865. No start date is fixed.
   - Fix: either the account claims only what exists by February 1863 (posted prices, C6's limits, the December talks, the slaving ban as an order), or CR-061 adds a dated 1862 order.

1. **Denver is missing from CR-060.** CR-067 B7: Great Basin under "James W. Denver … with Jack Hays as his colonel". CR-060 has Hays under "written orders from Monterey", and decision 1 reads "Hays commands". Say who orders the Bear River march and who signs the Compact. The department also covers "the Salt Lake road and Washoe" from October 1862, when Washoe is Union. B7's "OPEN there" for Marcus is stale.
2. **War resolution.** CR-067 A1: "a new theatre … needs a new resolution". CR-060 l.183: Elias gets the road "without asking Hawaii or the Assembly for a vote". Salt Lake 1862, Washoe 1864 and the Rio Grande column need a resolution or A1 needs narrowing.
3. **Hays.** D4 says he "made the Pyramid Lake peace with Numaga (canon §8)". Canon says only that he "defeats Paiute forces"; the bible says he helped "precipitate diplomatic disaster". CR-061 l.201 calls him "the name Victoria and Sherman would oppose". Correct D4 and give Monterey a stated reason.
4. **Volunteers.** CR-067 B6: volunteers "paid by the Treasury: the Washoe volunteers of 1860". CR-068 l.239: "no volunteer company ever paid from the treasury". CR-060 N1: "no volunteer companies". Define the ban (settler Indian-fighting companies) and say who paid Hays in 1860.

## Literary effect

1. **Nobody is home (CR-060 D5, D8; CR-067 B3).** Elias and Marcus are both away for the Compact and Bear River, yet Part II credits Elias with the choice. Give the decision to a named person on the spot (Denver, Stone or Beale) and let it cost him.
2. **Bear River (D8–D9).** Historically the lodges stood in the ravine (my knowledge), so a morning's fight there with "nobody in the camp" killed is hard to stage. Move the fight to the crossing. Also say why bands who "refuse" in January give up the killers in July.
3. **Hays (D4).** His restraint is the doctrine's hinge, and nothing explains why the man of 1860 obeys. One line of motive, such as what Washoe cost him, would earn it.
4. **Victoria's lost condition (C6).** She fails, and Washoe votes "all the same", so the defeat costs nothing. Let the refusal have a consequence she feels.
5. **The Owyhee (M8–M9).** A district whose press "preaches extermination" has a code admitting Paiute testimony, and its committee "threatens the court and does not act". Show who forces the testimony in and why the committee backs down.
6. **Deseret's entry (D11–D12).** The obstacle of 1862, a polygamous state before Hawaii and the Assembly, vanishes in 1867 offstage. Stage the vote, with Young's resented price beside it.
