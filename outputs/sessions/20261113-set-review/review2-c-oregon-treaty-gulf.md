## Blockers (new)

1. **CR-059 W15 and W13 against I2, W14 and the §10 text (l.130–132, 139, 598).** The author's fix note already lists this as awaiting him, but it is load-bearing.
   - W15: "Congress recognizes a state of war … before it rises on 6 August 1861".
   - W13: Washington ships four regiments to the Sound.
   - I2: "On 1 October 1861 the War Department orders every regular on the coast east" (Hist.).
   - Why it conflicts: the historical order assumed California volunteers would replace the regulars; here it abandons a theatre the Union is reinforcing.
   - Fix: make it this timeline's order (the interior regulars cannot be supplied and are to march east when the Mullan Road opens) and move the OR quotation to the historical baseline.

2. **The Oregon protocol's date (CR-059 l.603 storyline; CR-062 l.25).**
   - Both say "a protocol signed with the armistice".
   - CR-059 V1 (l.154): "the armistice protocol signed with it [the treaty]"; CR-062 O3: "signed with the treaty as a supplement to the 1865 armistice convention".
   - Why it conflicts: the armistice is June 1865 and the treaty December 1865, so storyline prose bound for canon misdates a ledger instrument.
   - Fix: "a protocol signed with the treaty, under the armistice".

3. **The Amity treaty against the June armistice (CR-062 A6 l.45, Part I l.165, ledger l.369).** Also listed as awaiting the author; the lapse decision of 13 November sharpened it.
   - Draft: "signed after Pacifica's armistice".

   - CR-068 CT23: recognition "waits on no armistice".

   - Fix: sign Amity in April–May, before Pacifica's armistice, with A2 drafted against the Amity text; delete "signed after" in all three places.

## Literary effect

1. **Elias's three temptations are clauses, not scenes.** Nobody presses him. Give (b) a petitioner (Nesmith, or Jackson County's delegates, asking for the testimony clause) and (c) a messenger with the early returns and an adviser who says "wait a day".
2. **Victoria wins X2 unopposed.** "Who resists" is still open, so the scene has no conflict. Let Deady or the convention's committee refuse first, or let Elias fear for Marion County, and show what she trades.
3. **Lincoln is "right and loses" twice, and the 1866 letter moves no votes by construction.** Let it cost something visible: a county, Nesmith's standing after Elias's concession, or a narrower exclusion vote.
4. **Logan's refusal "for a better offer" removes the test.** Stage it from his view: the Richmond officer names Mobile, and the consul sees only the refusal. The reader then knows more than Washington does.
5. **The Senate paragraph is one motive per senator, and Russia's signature arrives without a Pacifican risk.** Give de la Guerra a moment when Gorchakov proposes to wait for a Union bid, answered with the gold already at San Francisco.
6. **At Vicksburg the hinge belongs to Pemberton.** Marcus and Logan are unplaced on the 18th. Put Marcus at the broken bridge, where his paper allocation fails in front of him, and Logan burning the workshop within sight of Steele's skirmishers.
