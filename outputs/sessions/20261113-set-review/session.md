# Fresh-context review of the nine unintegrated drafts (CR-059 to CR-065, CR-067, CR-068)

## Brief

- **Desired outcome:** an independent verdict on whether the set can be integrated together, after the decisions and write-ins of 12 November, which had only the drafter's own review.
- **Authorization (author, 13 November 2026):** "Yes please" to starting the review with separate reviewer agents.
- **Method:** five read-only reviewers, each given only AGENTS.md, its drafts, a verbatim extract of canon §§8–13 and named rows of neighbouring drafts. None saw `outputs/` or CR-068-record.md. Each was told that dated author decisions are settled and to check the 12 November passages with particular care. Reports are saved verbatim: `review-1-oregon-treaty.md`, `review-2-interior-army.md`, `review-3-union-war-election.md`, `review-4-cr068.md`, `review-5-merged-storyline.md`.

## Verdict

All five say the set is **not ready to integrate**. No reviewer asks for an author decision to be reopened. Most findings are of one kind: a decision was written into its main row and not carried through the rows, tables and storyline text that depend on it. A smaller group needs the author.

## The merged storyline

Review 5 gives a merge map of every targeted canon paragraph. The nine texts cannot be applied as written: §10's "A Pacifican California column" paragraph is targeted by four drafts and CR-066 Part E, two anchors vanish once other drafts are applied, and §12 reaches 1867 before the Enabling Law of 1865. Five events are told twice (Lincoln's two letters, the dissolution, the evacuations, Texas in February 1863). The merged text has to be composed as one piece, in the order review 5 proposes.
