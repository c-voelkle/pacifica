## Resume here

The central case stands on six dated yield innovations with nitrogen from about 1900 (author, 18:41; MODEL). Yields are 1.37 times history's in 1910, 1.85 in 1920, 1.93 in 1930. Without research the Realm outgrows its soil in 1918; in the central case it grows food for 20 million more people than it has in 1920 and 11 million more in 1932, and outgrows soil and sea about 1941–43; the century's need is about nine times the home ceiling. Nitrogen needed: about 225 thousand tonnes a year by 1910 and 440 thousand by 1915, against the 400–480 thousand in all Chile's exports and CR-054's 90 thousand in 1929. Japan's rice gap in canon is about 8 million koku in 1932 (12%), with no colony to fill it. At 18:52 the author made Julian a provisioner: his tables slow to 2.0% a year over a century (the script's reading; the regency's stay at 1.5%), which gives 562 million in the Realm a century after 1930 and a need twelve times the home ceiling. Japan's direction: yields 5% above history's by 1910, 8% by 1920 and 13% by 1932 close its gap, a requirement for the partners' CR. At 19:09 the author fixed the slowing (1.5% and 2.0%, a story assumption), made Victoria a sufficer and named the theatres; C4 of the outline assesses each with sourced figures and proposes R-1 to R-7.
