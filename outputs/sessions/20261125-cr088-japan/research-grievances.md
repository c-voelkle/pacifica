# CR-088 research, step 8: the catalogue of Japanese grievances against Pacifica (1853–1932)

Built on 25 November 2026 from a sweep of `master_storyline.md` on `main` at `5d281dc` (every sentence that names Japan or a Japanese actor beside a word of refusal, limit, loss, resentment or protest; about 130 sentences), the CR-088 draft (revision 5) and our timeline's history. It is research for `A5` of `CR-088`, not canon. Line numbers are those of the storyline at `5d281dc`.

**Status words.**
- **Resolved:** the grievance ends, and canon says how.
- **Contained:** it lasts, but something weighs more against it, and canon or the draft says what.
- **Unresolvable:** it lasts for the people who hold it, whatever is done.
- **Latent:** it would be a grievance, but the Japanese do not know it.

The column "for `A5`" is the drafter's proposal.
