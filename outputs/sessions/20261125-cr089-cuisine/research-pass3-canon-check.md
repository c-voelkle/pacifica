## Canon passages the findings touch

- **The Palace Hotel (`L757 ("The Palace opens in October with every room let")`, PROPOSED, `CR-073`).** Consistent with RITZ ch5.6: D'Oyly Carte took San Francisco's Palace of 1875 as his model for the Savoy. In canon Ralston lives, so the Palace is his. This is a strong anchor for the destination strand.
- **Pacifican advisers in Kuroda's Hokkaido (`L745`, `CR-085`).** Consistent with CWI p.62: in our timeline a Maine canner under the Capron mission set up Hokkaido's first salmon canneries (1877–78). In canon those advisers can be Pacifican (PROPOSED).
- **Japan's rice never falls short (`CR-085`).** LEE ch9's interwar programmes after the rice riots of 1918 (synthetic sake, chemical soy sauce) are weaker or absent in canon's Japan; Suzuki Umetarō's vitamin work of 1910 stands.
- **No national Prohibition in the Union (`CR-076`).** LEV ch. 15's account of Prohibition breaking the Union's French restaurants does not apply; the Union's shift to cheap cosmopolitan restaurants comes from its middle class (HAL), more slowly.
- **Hoover as Pacifica's food administrator (`CR-051`).** LEV ch. 11 and REES ch13.11 describe his American administration's methods (pledges; licensing and reporting of cold stores). The second fits the Realm; the first does not (see Q5).
- **Szilárd inside the Works from about 1924 (`CR-050`).** REES ch15.2: his refrigerator patents with Einstein (1924–34). A small, consistent detail if wanted.
- **Prince Chichibu educated in Pacifica in 1925–26, his outdoorsman reputation made there (canon, AGENTS.md "Current canon").** Relevant to the destination strand as a royal patron (synthesis, part C).
- **Drink (`CR-076`): never glamour, rank or romance; the casino resort is at Guaymas, under Mexico's law (`L1034`).** A constraint on the destination strand: the Realm's luxury cannot be Monte Carlo's.
- **Tourism in canon:** apart from the Palace and Guaymas, the storyline says nothing about visitors, resorts or hotels. The destination strand is genuinely open.
