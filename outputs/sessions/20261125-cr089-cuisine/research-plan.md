## The questions (one notes file each in pass 2)

- **Q1. The cold chain.** Which refrigeration technologies existed when; where an electrified, ammonia-rich Realm with the world's fruit coast could lead (ammonia machines, refrigerated cars, cold storage, household refrigerators, quick-frozen food); what consumers distrusted and why.
- **Q2. The profession.** How French cooks made cooking a profession (schools, journals, associations, exhibitions, credentials); what the guild masters, fellowships and contests of `CR-089` A4 should borrow or avoid.
- **Q3. The grand hotel and the leisure class.** What Ritz and Escoffier invented (the hotel as a stage, à la carte, the brigade, electric light, private baths, dining for women); how seasons and destinations worked; what would bring the world's elite to Pacifica, and when.
- **Q4. The restaurant and the middle class.** Menus, prices, language, tipping, women, ethnic restaurants; what a transparency-minded Realm does differently.
- **Q5. Science and the table.** The New Nutrition, calories, home economics, vitamins, Hoover's Food Administration, processors and brands; how a Realm without a planned diet still measures and publishes.
- **Q6. Japan's modern kitchen.** Western food in Japan, the navy's diet, hotels and department stores, empire, Japanese food abroad; the traffic with the Realm.
- **Q7. Fermentation.** Koji and shōyu, the brewing institutes, Takamine, glutamate; what a Pacifican fermentation industry and science look like.
- **Q8. The prestige destination.** Pulled together from Q3 and the other notes: who comes, when, to what, and why it is Pacifica.

## The passes

1. **Keyword sweep** of all eight books against one query list (`research-pass1-sweep.md`), with a reading guide.
2. **Close reading** of the densest chapters, notes per question in `research-notes/` (paraphrase, page or segment, quotes under fifteen words only where the wording matters, and what it means for Pacifica).
3. **Canon check:** `CR-089` revision 3's *Hist.* claims and the storyline's food, hotel and travel passages against the books (`research-pass3-canon-check.md`).
4. **Synthesis:** an outline of the innovations Pacifica could champion and the prestige-destination strand (`research-synthesis.md`), as proposals for the author. Nothing in `CR-089` or canon changes until the author decides.
