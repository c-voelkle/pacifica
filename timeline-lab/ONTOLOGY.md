# Executable ontology for the 1927–28 dramatic sketch

Engine and scenario schema 0.2 implement a bounded subset of the revision 0.10 semantic design. Closed Pydantic records reject extra keys and nonfinite numbers. Evidence records carry stable IDs within a history, the immutable scenario hash as revision_id, provenance references and LAB status. The complete experiment is identified by scenario hash, engine version, world/seed, levers and branch specification. Replay also requires the pinned runtime dependencies.

## Identity and capacity

Entity distinguishes an acting person, an institution, the executive office and separately identified people within an institution. Descriptive people gain no controller. DecisionRecord and DecisionContext state who acts and the capacity in which the action is recorded. Role bindings and stable character registry IDs remain scenario content; a name is not a substitute for identity.

## Knowledge and intention

WorldFact is adjudicator state. Assertion is a speaker's claim. Observation is the immutable factual payload actually delivered to a named actor. Belief cites the observations that support its recipient-specific update. A private assurance creates no observation for outsiders. A public report remains a report, not universally accepted truth.

Goal declares a selected personal or institutional objective and its bounded mechanism binding. Commitment is a nonbinding political assurance with promisor, recipient, terms and status; it supplies no legal authority. Plan retains an assurance's active/achieved/frustrated actual status; subjective owner assessment remains unknown rather than being invented. Other goal projections retain unknown plan status where no executed intention assessment exists.

## Choice and execution

DecisionRecord captures delivered information, private inputs, goals, commitments, selected action, the bounded alternative catalog, actual procedure reason and random address. It does not claim an exhaustive planner evaluated uncomputed payoffs for all alternatives. In this slice the executive's institutional decisions act through the office identity; mediation remains the person's decision.

ActionAttempt and Adjudication link the choice to execution or a superseded-offer rejection. Actual legal status is explicitly not_assessed unless the specific illegality is modeled. RealizedEvent retains participant, time, facts and audience. Empty causal support is not an invented explanation. Registered actions and the original finance resolver remain bounded; a general reservation/settlement engine is deferred.

## Derived author views

StoryCandidate is derived from actual decisions/events. It carries its causal signature, supported fields, unassessed sacrifice where appropriate, assumptions and evidence references. Its next dilemma is a writing prompt, not a claim that the next event occurred. A forced policy-from-month branch is mechanical_consequence evidence; its history is not relabeled as an independently generated actor choice.

MetricDefinition supplies the name, units, owner role, definition, input references, producing mechanism and coverage. MetricSample binds a value or explicit missing reason to scenario/engine/world/seed and event evidence. A metric is not actor information unless an observation channel delivers it. Trust/influence are LAB proxies; no vote margin, Treasury cash or precise import cover is fabricated.

StoryBookmark embeds the immutable scenario, history, ontology evidence and a candidate plus author rationale. Saved bookmarks do not overwrite differing existing records. Downloaded bundles replay without an external service and reject changed narratives or inputs. Server-side files are not durable storage on all hosting services; the portable downloaded bundle is the retention mechanism there.

## Deliberate boundaries

This is not implementation of all 29 v0.10 record contracts or full R0 acceptance. General proposition/predicate identity, arbitrary temporal queries, all conduct/intervention modes, source extraction/omission audits, shared resource reservations, detailed financial ledgers and multigeneration branches remain in the backlog. Future authored story outcomes stay comparison references. Protected ambiguities remain outside discoverable state; no hidden culprit or secret is sampled.
